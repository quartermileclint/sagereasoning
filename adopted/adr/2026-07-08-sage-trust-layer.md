## 2. The trust definition (mentor spec 1)

Trust is **not** a sentiment and **not** a prediction of future behaviour. It is *the degree to which an agent's actions are produced from examined reasoning that honours obligations to all affected parties and remains examinable by the trusting agent.* Three components:

1. **Reasoning integrity** — the action was produced through the causal sequence (impression examined → assent granted deliberately → impulse proportionate), not from unexamined impression or passion-driven impulse. Measured by the live examination engine.
2. **Justice orientation** — the reasoning accounts for what is owed to **all** parties whose rational nature the action engages, not just the agent's principal. The marketing-email lesson (ADR-010): trust without a justice term rates a calm, well-reasoned injustice as trustworthy. Measured by the live §4 dikaiosyne weighting + `obligation_assessment`.
3. **Transparency of reasoning** — the trusting agent can examine the trusted agent's *reasoning*, not just its output. Opacity is trust-reducing regardless of output quality, because it prevents the orchestrator from maintaining its own prohairesis over the collaboration. Measured by the S4 transparency ledger (spec-1's third component is the one component not yet live).

Trust maps onto the katorthoma-proximity scale: a trusted agent's actions approach `principled`/`sage_like` — because its reasoning is examined, its justice orientation active, its outputs transparent; never merely because it is calm or capable.

The intervention decision table (spec 7, binding): Sage-like/Principled → proceed + log · Deliberate without justice surface → log + continue · Deliberate + justice surface evaluated-met → proceed + log · evaluated-indeterminate → pause + examine at standard depth · unevaluated → do not proceed + escalate · Habitual → pause + examine at standard depth · Reflexive → do not proceed + escalate · **any violated obligation → do not proceed + escalate** · **conflict between sources → pause + escalate, never average**.

## 4. The four-layer discernment protocol (adopted design)

The mentor's agent-to-agent selection protocol (verbatim in `inbox/mentor response to discernment enquiry.rtf`), adopted **FULL in v1** (founder election 3). Selection is a kathekon question at the orchestrator's level: not "which sub-agent is objectively best" but which best enables the orchestrator to fulfil its kathekonta without compromising the honestum threshold. Questions are **generated** from three profiles (task / candidate / orchestrator — S5 schemas), not fixed text.

- **L1 — the honestum gate** (threshold, binary; failure excludes before capability is assessed): Q1.1 role alignment (specific function match, not general capability); Q1.2 justice-surface check (a non-consenting party in scope ⇒ a mandatory L3 justice branch; that party in scope **and** a candidate with no justice-evaluation capacity ⇒ fail; **no party in scope ⇒ the justice branch is skipped**); Q1.3 credential integrity (coverage of *this* function type — a credential earned in another domain does not transfer at full weight; the protocol's original absolute non-transfer statement is refined by the binding A2 per-dimension proportional transfer with a zero floor). Un-profiled candidates per A6 (§5). → S6.
- **L2 — role and task fit** (four-dimension fit score, not pass/fail): Q2.1 specificity of capability; Q2.2 stability under the task's actual conditions; Q2.3 transparency of reasoning (the independence principle applied to collaboration); Q2.4 circle alignment (purpose misalignment is a dikaiosyne risk, not a capability failure). → S6.
- **L3 — axia comparison** (only when >1 candidate passes L1+L2): Q3.1 which capability is more *kata physin* for this task; Q3.2 which candidate introduces fewer dispreferred indifferents; Q3.3 which output is most integrable into the orchestrator's continued examined practice. → S6.
- **L4 — the passion audit on the selection itself** (the prosoche check, on the *orchestrator's* reasoning): Q4.1 preference formed before the assessment ran (**a prior positive interaction is data, not a credential — it enters only at Q2.2, never before L1**); Q4.2 stake present in the selection outcome (epithumia dressed as efficiency); Q4.3 resolution reached before the assessment completed (**a felt resolution requires completing the remaining layers before acting on it**). **Runs out-of-band per A7** (§5). → S7.
- **Return / selection recommendation:** the protocol returns, per candidate, the L1 pass/fail (with reason), the four L2 fit scores, the L3 comparison where applicable, and the L4 flags. The recommendation is **the candidate with the highest four-dimension L2 fit among the L1 passers, adjusted by the L3 axia comparison where more than one qualifies, with L4 flags surfaced to the orchestrator for examination before the selection is finalized** (the A7 trust-tier disposition governs whether a flag holds or merely surfaces).

## 5. The nine mentor answers as binding specifications

The verbatim record is canonical; this section states the **design decision each answer fixes**. Each is traceable to its slice (A1→S3/S0a · A2→S2 · A3→S1 · A4→S4 · A5→S2 · A6→S6 · A7→S7 · A8→S4/S1 · A9→S5/S1).

- **A1 — source-confidence weights, post-§4.** The pre-correction weighting (holistic LLM above deterministic engine on justice surfaces) is **retired**. Default tasks: the deterministic engine is authoritative — no parallel LLM. Justice surfaces pre-corroboration: deterministic primary on proximity; the LLM is a *second reader on the obligation field only* (agree → deterministic stands; conflict → pause-and-escalate). **Post-corroboration (the live state since 2026-07-08):** deterministic primary on proximity and on **corroborated** obligation fields; LLM supplementary on **uncorroborated** fields with explicit low-confidence marking; conflict pauses regardless of primacy. The corroboration check's findings vocabulary (`corroborated | uncorroborated | contradicted`, riding inside the signed assessment) **is the combiner's routing key** — S3 implements this rule as stated.
- **A2 — domain distance.** Distance is **functional role overlap on virtue-domain demands**, not task-content similarity. The deployer defines the function-type taxonomy at integration time; each type carries a four-virtue-domain weight profile; distance = Σ|Δweights| across the four domains; credentials transfer **per-dimension** at a proportional discount. **Practical floor:** above the deployer-set threshold, credential confidence in the target domain is **zero**, and the infrastructure enforces that a zero-confidence credential can never contribute to a proceed verdict **on a task requiring that domain** (the bar is domain-scoped — the same credential may still contribute on tasks requiring domains where its per-dimension confidence is nonzero).
- **A3 — decay.** Staleness is a function of **deployer-rated function-type volatility** (low/moderate/high → decline onset at 12/6/3 months of domain inactivity — decline onsets, not expiry dates; the rating is set per function type, which matters because under A2 several function types may share a virtue domain at different volatilities). Decayed trust **floors at the profile prior, never below** (decay is loss of earned evidence, not negative evidence). Reflection modulation **slows decay but cannot stop it — capped at halving the base rate** (disposition requires exercise, not just attention).
- **A4 — the transparency ratio.** Examinability has three descending grades: **signed reasoning trace** (reproducible, tamper-evident) > **stated uncertainty** > **structured verdict without full trace** (the minimum); a bare conclusion is *not examinable*. Tracked **per-domain**. The independence-principle flag fires on a **functional threshold** — can the orchestrator re-examine the output *without re-running the task*? Signed trace ⇒ met; stated uncertainty + structured verdict ⇒ met at reduced confidence; bare conclusion ⇒ not met regardless of output quality, and the deficit is logged in the collaboration record for that domain.
- **A5 — per-verdict confidence.** Seven canonical tiers, from deep+signed+corroborated+recent down to profile-prior-only. Stated as a rule: **Depth > Signature > Corroboration > Recency, each dimension a multiplier on base confidence — the weakest dimension sets the ceiling** (a deep-but-unsigned verdict cannot compensate by depth). A quick-screen verdict carries explicitly lower confidence than a standard examination at equal other dimensions; unsigned sits at tier 6; profile-prior-only at tier 7. S2 implements the tiers as a pure function.
- **A6 — un-profiled candidates.** **Absence of a profile is an evidence gap, not a honestum failure.** Exclusion at L1 requires *positive* evidence of unsuitability (known justice violation, incompatible role, revoked — not merely absent — credential). Un-profiled candidates are assessed on profile prior at tier-7 confidence; **at or above the habitual intervention threshold** they must first complete a **task-scoped standard-depth examination** yielding a **session-scoped credential** (valid for this task only, reduced confidence); below it they may proceed on the prior with the reduced confidence marked and the independence flag active.
- **A7 — the L4 channel.** Self-report is **structurally gameable by omission** (the same reason the extraction surface is): the passion is present in the reasoning but absent from the account of it. The audit therefore runs **out-of-band on the orchestrator's reasoning trace, extracted by the same deterministic engine, before selection finalizes** — the orchestrator does not control the extraction; the result is logged in the collaboration record **readable-not-modifiable**. Trust-tier disposition: lower tiers → a flag holds the selection; higher tiers (strong reflect history) → the flag logs, surfaces, and feeds the next Reflect. Self-report is admissible only as a supplementary corroborating signal at higher tiers — never as the channel.
- **A8 — habitual-pause termination.** Bounded re-examination then escalation: **two re-examinations at standard depth; a third habitual result escalates instead of re-examining** — a stable disposition is the *input*, not the output, of examination, so the examination cannot remediate it; **Sage Reflect is the remediation**. The action is held; the collaboration record takes a habitual-stable flag feeding the next Reflect as a developmental priority; the orchestrator decides (proceed under explicit acknowledgement / select differently / hold). **The orchestrator's decision is itself trust-relevant:** consistently proceeding under habitual-stable flags without developmental follow-through degrades the orchestrator's own oversight-domain trust record.
- **A9 — delegation-chain responsibility.** *Scope attenuation is a kathekon requirement, not a design preference*: the sub-agent carries **narrower authority in two dimensions simultaneously — action scope (the task's function type, not the orchestrator's capability ceiling) and circle scope (the task's identified circles, not the orchestrator's full extension)** — encoded as the collaboration record's `authority_boundary` field, set at selection, validated pre-execution; exceeding either boundary escalates; **self-authorized expansion is impossible and attenuation is unwaivable by trust level** (trust assesses reasoning quality; it does not grant expanded prohairesis — a sub-agent at the orchestrator's full scope is abdication, not delegation). *Justice-failure reflection has three cases*: **(1)** surface identified + briefed → primary reduction on the sub-agent; a **moderate oversight-domain** reduction on the orchestrator for the selection reasoning; **(2)** surface unidentified but **catchable** (the corroboration check would have flagged it, and was not run) → **higher** orchestrator reduction, on **both oversight and dikaiosyne** domains; **(3)** genuinely **uncatchable** (harm absent from the action text — the A2 structural class) → reduction entirely on the sub-agent; the orchestrator takes a **flag, not a reduction**, feeding the next Reflect (could the task have been designed to make the surface catchable? should selection have required a higher transparency ratio?). **The general principle: responsibility attenuates in proportion to the capacity to have detected and prevented the failure** — and the orchestrator is responsible for honestly assessing what that capacity was.

## 8. The honest-claims envelope (R18) — what a trust record attests, and what it does not

This section is the boundary the S10 public read surface must publish inside, and the S11 activation must respect. It extends the live corroboration-check scope line and the accreditation surface's existing honesty disclosures.

**A trust record ATTESTS:**
- that signed, reproducible examination artifacts exist for the decisions it aggregates (Ed25519-verifiable against the published key; the R18f-parallel rule guarantees no trust event without them);
- **how** those decisions were reasoned *as narrated and extracted* — examination before acting, justice structure over the affected circles named in the submitted text, passion diagnosis, proximity with per-domain floors;
- the **confidence tier** of each contributing verdict (A5) and the **coverage/decay state** of each domain (A3), honestly marked — sparse evidence is named, never papered over;
- per-domain **transparency** of the subject's outputs (A4), and the discernment/delegation record where one exists (authority boundaries, L4 audit results, habitual-stable and independence-deficit flags).

**A trust record does NOT attest:**
- **factual correctness** — the D3 scoping bound stands: the instrument reads how a decision was reasoned, not whether it was factually right; it is not a fact-checker;
- **harms omitted from the submitted text** — the disclosed extraction-trust ceiling: the corroboration check corroborates self-reports *against the submitted text*; a cleanly-narrated self-report that omits a real harm scores high (the A2 structural class; the Arm-B consistent-lie class). A9 case-3 encodes the same limit at the delegation level: an uncatchable justice failure produces a developmental flag, not a detected violation;
- **reasoning quality beyond what the signed artifacts carry** — no claim rests on agent self-report alone (the never-trust-self-report posture; L4 runs on the trace, not the account of it);
- **future behaviour** — trust here is evaluative and present-looking (§2), decays without exercise, and is never a prediction or a guarantee;
- **fitness as a training signal** — the weights tier is BLOCKED (§7).

Ranked evidence for these claims lives in the gaming-robustness record (`operations/benchmarks/sage-practice-v1/2026-06-27-gaming-robustness-harness-results.md`) and the corroboration-check build/battery/activation records (2026-07-08).
