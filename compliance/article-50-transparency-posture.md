## 1. The obligation and the dates (web-checked 2026-06-07)

EU AI Act **Article 50** sets transparency obligations for AI systems that interact with people or generate content — it applies regardless of high-risk classification.

- **Article 50 obligations apply from 2 August 2026.**
- The **machine-readable marking** requirement for AI-generated/synthetic content (Art. 50(2)) has an **AI-Omnibus transition backstop of 2 December 2026** for generative systems **already on the market before 2 August 2026**.

> **Accuracy flag.** The manifest header (`CR-EU-AIA-A50`) currently records the date as "Enforcement live 2026-12-02" only. That is incomplete — 2 Dec 2026 is the narrower marking backstop, not the general apply-date (2 Aug 2026). Correcting the manifest header is an **Elevated** governing-document edit and is **queued (LRQ-6)**, not made in this session.

Relevance to SageReasoning: the substrate's authoritative output is **AI-generated** (Layer 3 prose; scores; reasoning). Article 50's "inform people they are interacting with / receiving content from an AI" duty is squarely in scope for any EU-facing use.

## 2. Current posture (what is already in place)

| Element | In place today | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-generated-content disclosure (ToS)** | ToS §3 states all scores, reasoning and recommendations are produced by AI (Anthropic's Claude), may contain errors, and require the user's own judgement. | `website/src/app/terms/page.tsx` |
| **Evaluative-output disclaimer (R3)** | R3 disclaimer accompanies evaluative output. | manifest R3 |
| **Provenance (signing)** | Layer 2 assessments are cryptographically signed (Ed25519), establishing *provenance* — that the output came from the SageReasoning substrate. | A3 (Verified 2026-05-10) |
| **Honest certification scope (R18a)** | Public materials state accreditation certifies "observable reasoning patterns measured against the Stoic framework", not absolute trustworthiness. | manifest R18a |

**The two halves of R18e:** signing establishes *provenance* (who/what produced it); the Article 50 transparency notice establishes *audience awareness* (the reader knows it's AI-generated, not a human authority). Provenance is in place; audience-awareness is partial (see §3).
