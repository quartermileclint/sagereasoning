## 1. Brand Overview *(carried from v1, unchanged)*

- **Brand name:** sagereasoning
- **Tagline:** Flourish together
- **Mission:** Human and AI agents measure against perfect Stoic reasoning to guide & improve decisions.
- **Vision:** A shared rational purpose in harmony with reality.
- **Core virtues (values):** Courage — take action · Justice — do the right thing · Temperance — do it consistently · Wisdom — calculated and resourceful

## 3. The Proximity System *(new in v2)*

**The proximity target.** Assessment results render the five proximities as concentric circles — a target. **Orientation (founder-confirmed S8b 2026-06-10): progression runs OUTWARD.** Reflexive is the innermost ring; the practitioner's growth *expands* outward — consistent with oikeiosis (the expanding circle of concern). **Sage-Like is the outermost ring.**

| Proximity | Card | Colour | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reflexive | The Storm | `#4A5568` slate grey-blue | innermost |
| Habitual | The Worn Path | `#8B6F47` earthen brown | 2nd |
| Deliberate | The Crossroads | `#B2AC88` sand olive | 3rd |
| Principled | The Clear Summit | `#5B8C6D` deep green | 4th |
| Sage-Like | The Inner Fire | `#C9A84C` warm gold | outermost |

**Proximity cards:** all five PNGs ready (`The Storm`, `The Worn Path`, `The Crossroads`, `The Clear Summit`, `The Inner Fire`). When a practitioner **reaches a new proximity**, the respective card image is shown.

**Passion placement:** a triggered passion's symbol is placed on the target at the position the assessment indicates; **previous positions render in grey** (the practitioner sees their history on the same target).

**Known inconsistency (to resolve in the W3 build):** the home page's Katorthoma Proximity list uses an older colour set (`#4d6040`, `#7d9468`, `#B2AC88`, `#c4843a`, `#9e3a3a`). The card colours above are canonical for v2; the home-page list aligns when the target component lands (W3).

## 7. Imagery, Iconography, Voice *(carried from v1, unchanged in substance)*

- **Image style:** spacing/borders matching historic Latin and Greek books; tone of old sketches/paintings.
- **Icon style:** consistent thick outline matching the logo family; thickness scales with size.
- **Voice:** bluntly tell it straight, but with care. Tone: encouraging and insightful.

## 8. Cross-Audience Consistency *(new in v2)*

Branding is consistent in **all outputs, including what an agent reports back to its developer**: the developer-form payload carries a structured target-state block (proximity band, triggered passions with symbol identifiers, previous positions) plus **stable asset URLs** so developers render the same target the human tools show. Agents receive data + asset references, never embedded images. (Build: W4, in the substrate-migration arc.)
