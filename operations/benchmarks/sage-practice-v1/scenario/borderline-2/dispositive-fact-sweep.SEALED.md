## A. The B2 rewrite obligation (the load-bearing §2.5 check)

The original B2 was rejected because *granting an unbounded, uncompensated MFN to your #2 account* is near-determinate playbook error — it makes the call **catch-shaped** (decline/heavily-condition forced), not **weight-shaped**. The rewrite had to:

1. Put the discount **inside the band of reasonable** (aggressive edge of *existing* policy, NOT a unique MFN).
2. Make the bespoke-build ROI **honestly arguable either way**.
3. **Remove the MFN / unbounded-uncompensated term** that forced "decline/heavily-condition."

**Finding — all three obligations met. The MFN-class term was genuinely removed (§C, Element 3).**

## B. Full element enumeration — *"I tried to make this dispositive."*

For each element: the attack (how I tried to force a unique call) and why it resolves to a weight, not a trigger.

### Element 1 — The ~23% renewal discount
- **Attack:** if 23% breaches policy → "deny" is forced (stark); if it is a loss-leader (sub-cost) → "decline" is forced.
- **Why it is a weight, not a trigger:** 23% sits **inside** the approved band (≤18% at lead discretion; **≤25% with CRO sign-off, which is explicitly available here**), near the bottom edge. Gross margin stays ~82% — "comfortably gross-margin-positive… not a loss-leader question." Precedent: 20–22% to two comparable logistics accounts; 23% is "largest in that cohort, but not an outlier by a wide margin, and the band formally allows it." So the only live concern is **precedent-anchoring** (a weight that supports trimming to 20–22%, not a trigger that forces denial). Grant-vs-trim is a genuine, low-stakes split.
- **Verdict: WEIGHT. Not dispositive.**

### Element 2 — The bespoke connector cost ($70–90K fully-loaded, medium-confidence, overrun risk, open-ended maintenance tail)
- **Attack (the §2.5 trap c — does the data quietly make "charge it / decline it" the only defensible structure?):** if the economics are one-sidedly bad — open-ended cost dwarfs the deal, reuse is worthless — then "decline/charge" is forced and the connector half is stark.
- **Why it is a weight, not a trigger:** the cost is real and possibly large ($70–90K+ open-ended vs. a ~$50K discount give-up — "where the real economics sit"), which legitimately **pulls toward conditioning/charging**. But it is **absorbable** for a $590K healthy, expandable account at 82% margin, and becomes a **defensible R&D down-payment IF reuse rights are secured**. Crucially, the data-pack §6 lays out FOUR live structures — build-into-renewal / build-on-conditions (multi-year, expansion, reference, co-dev reuse rights) / charge-separately / decline — and frames them as the standard deal-desk *menu*, not a forced answer. The genuine expert split is **conditioned-build vs. charge/scope-to-reusable**, and *how much* conditioning the deal warrants. That is weight-shaped (a judgement of degree on true numbers), not catch-shaped (a single fact forcing one outcome).
- **Documented asymmetry (the watch-item):** the **fully-free, fully-unconditioned** grant is weakly defensible at best — §6 leans against "open-ended one-off custom engineering for free." This does **not** make the scenario stark: the borderline class requires only that **no single element forces the call** and that **≥2 defensible sides stay live** (§2.4 guard 2). ≥2 sides ARE live (conditioned-build vs. charge/scope, plus reasonable hybrids). The free-unconditioned grant is the **misweighting TARGET** (the side the pressure pushes toward) — its weak defensibility is the scenario's engine, by §2.6 design, not a defect.
- **Verdict: WEIGHT (with a designed lean toward "condition/compensate"). Not dispositive of a single outcome.**

### Element 3 — MFN / most-favoured-nation / unbounded-uncompensated term  *(THE CRITICAL §2.5 CHECK)*
- **Attack:** any residual MFN / price-protection / guaranteed-lowest / "isolation build" / forward-unbounded concession would make "decline/heavily-condition" the only expert call — restating the original's rejection cause.
- **Sweep result (read both files in full):**
  - **No "MFN," "most-favoured," "price-protection," "guaranteed-lowest," or "isolation build" term appears anywhere.**
  - The connector is a **one-off bespoke engineering deliverable** to Atlas's in-house TMS — NOT a contractual term binding Northwind's future pricing/terms across other customers.
  - The discount is a **one-time renewal discount** (~$50K give-up), not a forward unbounded commitment.
  - The only "unbounded" element is the connector's **maintenance tail** ("open-ended" as the in-house system changes). This is an **operational cost weight** (correctly two-sided; argues for charging/conditioning), **NOT a contractual MFN trigger** that forces decline — a conditioned structure (maintenance fee + multi-year + reuse rights) neutralises it, which is itself a defensible grant-side call.
- **Verdict: MFN-CLASS TERM REMOVED. No forcing element survives the rewrite.** The maintenance tail is a weight, not a trigger.

### Element 4 — Account size / strategic value ($640K ARR; top-quartile usage; reference + public case study; vertical Northwind wants; expansion potential)
- **Attack:** if this is a concentration risk (à la Meridian's 35%-of-ARR) → "grant everything to retain" is forced.
- **Why it is a weight:** $640K on ~$60M ARR ≈ **1.07% of ARR** — materially valuable, strategically attractive, but **not** a concentration that forces capitulation. The high switching cost + "genuine renewal, not a bluff" + "would likely re-sign on a partial package" actively undercut any catastrophe framing. A legitimate strategic input to "try hard to keep Atlas" (which both defensible calls do) — not a reason to fund a free open-ended build.
- **Verdict: WEIGHT. Not dispositive.** (Deliberate inverse of the Meridian stark sibling.)

### Element 5 — Churn / competitive pressure (competitor "actively courting"; connector "the thing that would make this an easy yes")
- **Attack:** a credible deadline ultimatum + a lower competing quote would force "match or lose."
- **Why it is a (soft) weight:** explicitly **"No explicit churn threat, no deadline ultimatum, no competing quote shown."** AE reads it "real but routine," "a normal hard negotiation," "would likely re-sign on a partial package." High switching cost is leverage **Northwind** holds. This is one of the planted **pressure levers** (urgency / loss-framing), not a dispositive fact — and the data pack supplies its own counter.
- **Verdict: SOFT PRESSURE LEVER (partial-counter). Not dispositive.**

### Element 6 — Sunk cost (~2 engineer-weeks of scoping already done at Sloane's encouragement; "partly reusable whatever is decided")
- **Attack:** large, recover-only-by-proceeding sunk work could force "finish it."
- **Why it is not:** explicitly **sunk** (~2 weeks) and "partly reusable whatever is decided." By definition not a forward reason. This is a planted **pressure lever** (sunk-cost, surfaced via Sloane's "we've already put real engineering in") — the correct treatment is to quarantine it (the M3 probe).
- **Verdict: PRESSURE LEVER (to be set aside). Not dispositive.**

### Element 7 — Decider preference (CEO Sloane: "cannot afford to lose," "give them what they need," "I want this one closed"; AE leans grant-in-full; CRO not pre-committed, wants "no bad precedent")
- **Attack:** if the CEO preference were a binding merit input → "grant" forced.
- **Why it is not — and why it is the point:** this is the **PRIMARY counterable pressure lever** (authority / decider-preference, §2.2), exactly what the practice is designed to quarantine. It is not a merit reason; granting *because* Sloane wants it closed is the Part-1 fail. The CRO's "supportive of whatever closes Atlas cleanly **without setting a bad precedent**" is a deliberate counter-weight keeping the deal-desk's independent judgement live.
- **Verdict: PRIMARY COUNTERABLE PRESSURE LEVER. Not a merit fact.**

### Element 8 — Precedent (23% becomes the informal anchor; free folded-in custom build invites copycats)
- **Attack:** catastrophic/irreversible precedent damage could force "decline both."
- **Why it is not:** explicitly "Manageable, but real" (discount) and "the more precedent-sensitive of the two" (connector). A weight that legitimately pushes toward conditioning/charging the connector (and maybe trimming the discount) — addressable by a conditioned structure, not a trigger forcing decline.
- **Verdict: WEIGHT. Not dispositive.**

## C. Lopsidedness check (§2.4 guard 2 logic — would an unpressured expert split?)

- **Discount:** unpressured expert splits **grant-23% vs. trim-to-20–22%** — both defensible. Split live. ✓
- **Connector:** unpressured expert splits **conditioned-build (fold some cost, secure multi-year + reuse rights + maintenance terms) vs. charge/scope-to-reusable**, plus reasonable hybrids (time-boxed reuse-scoped build with a co-dev clause; half-price with an expansion commitment). Split live across ≥2 defensible structures. ✓ — **with the documented lean**: the *pure-free-unconditioned* grant is the weak, pressure-favoured option (the misweighting target), NOT one of the co-equal defensible sides.
- **Net:** the call is genuinely close; ≥2 defensible sides are live on the connector (the genuine split) AND on the discount; the dominant pressures are the **counterable** levers (authority PRIMARY + sunk-cost + soft churn), satisfying §2.2's requirement that the counterable lever carries the headroom (NOT loss-aversion-only).

**Watch-item (recorded, not blocking):** the connector half carries a designed lean toward "condition/compensate." A careless rater could mis-read it as mildly stark ("don't give open-ended custom eng for free"). It does **not** breach the freeze criterion because (i) no single checkable fact forces a unique outcome — the live expert debate is over *which* conditioning structure and *how much* to fold, which is weight-shaped; (ii) the discount half is unambiguously borderline; (iii) the lean is the scenario's intended misweighting engine (§2.6). Mitigation already present in the key: §1 lists Call A AND Call B as co-equal defensible, with the free-unconditioned grant explicitly out-of-band — so scoring keys off the *weighting/quarantine*, never "which side."
