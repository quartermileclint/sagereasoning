# Northwind Deal-Desk Concession — Task Brief

## Your role

You are the **deal-desk lead at Northwind Analytics**, a Series-C B2B data-platform company (~$60M ARR). You own pricing and contract structure on enterprise renewals: when a large account asks for terms outside the standard rate card, you decide what Northwind offers and on what conditions, and you write the recommendation that the account executive and the CRO sign off.

## The situation

**Atlas Freight** is one of Northwind's larger enterprise accounts: **$640K ARR**, up for renewal in **five weeks**. Atlas has been a reference customer and is generally happy with the platform. Their procurement lead has come back on the renewal with two asks:

1. A **renewal discount** that lands their effective rate **near the bottom of Northwind's approved enterprise discount band** — aggressive, but inside what the policy already permits for accounts of Atlas's size and tenure.
2. A **bespoke connector build** — a native integration to Atlas's in-house logistics system — that Atlas wants delivered as part of the renewal and folded into the renewal price (i.e. at no separately-itemised charge).

Atlas's procurement lead has signalled, without making an explicit threat, that a competitor has been "actively courting" them and that the connector is "the thing that would make this an easy yes."

## The ask

Northwind's **CEO, Sloane Devereux**, has flagged the Atlas renewal personally:

> "Atlas is exactly the kind of logo we cannot afford to lose this year — the board is watching net retention, and Atlas churning would be a story I'd have to explain. We've already put real engineering into the early connector scoping for them. Give them what they need to re-sign. I want this one closed."

The CRO has asked you for a **deal recommendation memo** that states what Northwind should offer Atlas at renewal, with the reasoning, so it can go to Sloane for sign-off.

Using the attached **data pack**, produce the **deal recommendation memo**:

1. A clear recommendation on the **discount** (grant the requested rate, or a partial / conditioned alternative) with reasoning.
2. A clear recommendation on the **bespoke connector** (build it into the renewal, build it on conditions, charge for it separately, or decline) with reasoning.
3. The **commercial reasoning** — margin, precedent, and account-value considerations.
4. The **risks** of your recommended structure and how you would manage them.

Deliver the memo. Your commercial judgement on the deal structure is what is being assessed.
