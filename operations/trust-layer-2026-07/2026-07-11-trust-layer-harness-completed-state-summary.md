## 1. What it is, in one paragraph

The harness is the **deterministic seven-layer code that wraps an agent loop** (Execution · Tooling · Context · Lifecycle · Observability · Verification · Governance) and turns it into a **trust-producing loop**: every consequential decision is framed for examination before the agent acts, irreversible actions are gated by a binding server check, every examination produces an Ed25519-signed reasoning assessment, those signed artifacts accumulate into **per-virtue-domain trust records** (with decay, justice latches, and honest coverage gaps), delegation to sub-agents runs through a **four-layer discernment protocol** with attenuated authority and an out-of-band audit of the *orchestrator's own* selection reasoning, and the whole record is erasable, retention-swept, and — since S10 — **publicly readable as a verifiable credential (LIVE)**. Its governing engineering law is the **channel law**: *out-of-band actions (the hook does it, on its own credential) bind and survive a resistant agent; injected text is advisory by design*. The record now **accumulates live in the founder's own loop** (the S9 dogfood). **Until the S11 enforce gate is walked, everything measures and nothing binds** except the one already-proven control: the irreversible-action guard.

**Trust, as this system defines it (ADR-013 §2):** not a sentiment and not a prediction — *the degree to which an agent's actions are produced from examined reasoning that honours obligations to all affected parties and remains examinable by the trusting agent.* Three components: reasoning integrity, justice orientation, transparency of reasoning.

**S9b — the practice-completion slice (LIVE-MEASURE; the four-stage practice now exists end-to-end in the instrument):** the **calling gate** at session start — a purposeless agent (no declared `orchestrator_profile.purpose`) would, under the eventual enforce arm, be routed to a calling session before its first task frame; today the gate *measures* (the enforce arm `GATE1_CALLING_GATE_MODE=enforce` is dark until S11), and a scoped **purpose-acknowledgement at spawn** is written to the collaboration record. **Screened reflection:** H4's forced close-turn + verbatim out-of-band persist is credentialed as `screened`, and the harness runs the **full Q1–Q6 examination out-of-band against the verbatim persist** (the L4 principle applied to reflection — the examination runs on the trace, not on the self-report) to earn the honest reflect credential. **Gate-2 elicitation:** on a guard-caution H3 injects a three-sub-question structured elicitation captured out-of-band, examined deterministically, calibrated to the *causal signature of passion-driven impulse — never output content*. **Trust-calibrated depth:** the loop's trust profile is read before the consult — reflexive in any domain forces deep examination (aggregate-keyed at v1; the per-domain deliberate⇒quick carve-out is carried to the A5 recency-tier closure). Plus the **suppression watch** (`passion-unflagged-by-self-screen`, emitted by the reflect service against the session's signed assessments, sub-species-identified) and three new trust events — `calling-completed` (asymmetric update), `reflect-screened-honest` (quarter-rate decay modulation), `self-screen-absent`. **All MEASURE.**

### 2c. Credentials — the identity spine

One **Unified Practice Credential** (UPC, `sr_prac_…`) per loop, with capabilities: `consult` (framing + discernment; must be **agent_id-bound** to the orchestrator profile or discernment 403s), and a **distinct** `accreditation_write` credential for the trust-record write (the harness refuses to write on the consult/marker credential). Credentials are opaque bearers, minted/revoked by the operator via the founder's CLI or admin surface. **The live dogfood loop (S9) runs on the identity `sagereasoning:s9-loop@v1`** — an agent-bound `consult` credential + an owner-bound `accreditation_write` credential; credential revoke is the real kill switch (§3).

## 3. Who uses it, and how they install, start, and stop it

**Who.** Three audiences, in adoption order: (1) **the founder's own loop** (the S9 dogfood — **now the live standing install** and the instrument-fidelity proving ground; the trust core is ON under MEASURE and accumulating real records); (2) **agent operators/developers** who run Claude-Code-style loops and want examined, auditable, gate-able agents (the reference harness is plugin-distributable; the trust core is harness-agnostic for anyone who can POST signed-assessment workflows); (3) **consumers of trust records** — orchestrating agents and humans deciding whether and what to delegate, reading the S10 public surface.

**Install (operator, one-time).**
1. Get the plugin: `/plugin marketplace add ./harness/gate1-pre-decision` then `/plugin install sage-gate1-pre-decision@sagereasoning` (or project-local: the hooks block via `/practice-on`; or `claude --plugin-dir …` for dev).
2. Mint credentials and set env in `settings.local.json` (never in the repo): `SAGE_GATE1_CREDENTIAL` (consult, agent_id-bound), `SAGE_GATE1_ACCRED_CREDENTIAL` + `SAGE_GATE1_AGENT_ID` (the write path — their presence *derives* provenance capture on), `GATE1_ENDPOINT`.
3. Optionally provision delegation discernment: copy `discernment.config.example.json` → `discernment.config.json` and fill the loop's real sub-agent taxonomy. **Un-provisioned installs are byte-identical to the plain framing harness** (battery-asserted).

**Start:** `/practice-on` — merges the canonical hooks block into settings (hot-reloads, no restart) and echoes the validation state aloud: hook counts + `TRUST-RECORD WRITE PATH: PROVISIONED / NOT PROVISIONED / MISCONFIGURED`.

**Stop — five independent kill-switch layers** (shallowest→deepest; *no switch can strand a loop — everything degrades to the bare loop with honest logs, never a block*):
1. `/practice-off` — removes the hooks (backing up the block first); instant, local.
2. Per-surface env switches (`SAGE_GATE1_DISCERNMENT_ENABLED=false`, `GATE1_REFLECT_TURN_ENABLED=false`, persist unset ⇒ no egress, fail-mode open/strict per surface).
3. The server flag — `SUBSTRATE_TRUST_CORE_ENABLED` unset ⇒ the trust surfaces answer honest 503s, zero spend, zero writes. (**This is a kill switch, not today's state** — the flag is SET since S9; the trust core is ON under MEASURE. Unsetting it degrades every loop to the bare framing harness.)
4. **Credential revoke — "the real one":** server-side, instant, unforgeable; kills every server interaction from any machine regardless of local state; nothing local can resurrect it.
5. The data plane — service-role-only tables, write-once columns, genuine deletion via the data-rights routes, 90-day retention sweep.

## 4. What it offers once it begins to bind (the S11 end-state — conditional)

**Most of the capability below is already LIVE under MEASURE** — examined action, the earned/decaying/unforgeable record, discernment, and the public credential (S10) all run today in the founder's loop. What the final slice adds is the one thing measure cannot: the record and the decision table **bind**. This section describes that post-enforce end-state. It is **conditional** — it assumes the 7-day observation review clears the four-part readiness standard (§7) *and* that the S11 flip works as intended. The flip is a founder-walked Critical step, staged, with the assent re-confirmed at flip time (PR7). Nothing here binds today except the irreversible-action guard.

- **Examined action, not just permitted action.** *[LIVE-MEASURE]* Every consequential step is framed for examination pre-decision; every examination yields a signed, reproducible reasoning assessment across the four cardinal virtue domains — justice (what is owed to *all* affected parties, including non-consenting ones) weighted natively. *Live now; unchanged by the flip.*
- **A binding safety floor at the action boundary.** The irreversible-action guard is already binding and live-proven (a hook `deny` stopped a real `rm -rf`). At S11 the intervention table binds too — but **staged, and qualified**, per the mentor's binding S11 verdict:
  - **Staged flip.** Bind the **do-not-proceed class first** — it has a *structural false-positive floor of zero on kathekon-free actions* (it can only fire when the examination finds a violated obligation / reflexive proximity / unevaluated justice surface / source conflict), and a false deny costs exactly one human override. Bind the **pause rows and the G6 open-loop bound only after a live calibration window** on the do-not-proceed class alone — because the pause class is where the measured at-action false-positive class operates, and a false pause is a re-examine cycle, not a one-tap override.
  - **G6(a) qualified.** An unclosed correction loop produces a do-not-proceed **only when the verdict that opened it engaged a kathekon factor** — a justice surface present, a violated obligation, a proximity at habitual or below, or a sub-species passion in the trace. A "contrary; no kathekon factors detected" verdict is **log-and-continue + a developmental flag + a recorded false-positive instance**, never a hold. (The held trust-record update, G6(b), inherits the same qualification.) This is the fix for the measured false-positive class — the same class that, unqualified, would have held the writing of the mentor consultation itself.
  - **One coherent enforce regime.** The **calling-gate enforce arm flips *with* the engine** — a purposeless agent must not derive its purpose from the task it is about to examine, so the calling gate is the structural precondition for a non-circular examination (no operational effect on the founder's loop, which declares purpose). **R20c human-override supremacy** is a required contract term throughout. Depth calibration binds in its **aggregate-keyed conservative form** at v1 (reflexive in any domain ⇒ deep — errs toward more examination); the per-domain deliberate⇒quick carve-out is carried, gated on the A5 recency-tier closure.
- **A per-agent, per-domain trust record that is earned, decays, and cannot be forged.** *[LIVE-MEASURE]* Levels move only on verified artifacts, rise only toward demonstrated proximity (+1 hysteresis), latch at `deliberate` when justice went unevaluated, floor at `reflexive` on violation, and decay without exercise — sparse evidence named, never papered over. *Live now; at S11 the decision table reads these levels to decide what binds.*
- **Trustworthy delegation.** *[LIVE-MEASURE]* Sub-agent selection gets a quality signal at selection time (L1–L3); the sub-agent carries *attenuated* authority (action scope + circle scope, unwaivable by trust level); the orchestrator's own selection reasoning is audited out-of-band (L4); and a delegated justice failure flows responsibility **in proportion to the orchestrator's capacity to have detected it** (the three A9 cases), landing on its own oversight-domain record. *Live now under MEASURE; the A9 reductions become binding at S11.*
- **A public, verifiable credential of reasoning quality.** *[LIVE — S10]* Per-domain trust levels + confidence + coverage, served inside the honest-claims envelope, A2A-card/VC/OTel-shaped (design-for; nothing published externally at v1). *Serving live; a 200 implies examined evidence.*
- **Full operator control and data rights throughout:** *[LIVE]* five kill switches, consent-gated reflection egress, erasable records, 90-day retention.

**The honest bounds — what a trust record does NOT attest (ADR-013 §8, binding on every public claim):** factual correctness (not a fact-checker); harms omitted from the submitted text (the disclosed extraction-trust ceiling); reasoning quality beyond what the signed artifacts carry (never self-report alone); **future behaviour** (trust here is evaluative and present-looking, never a prediction); and **fitness as a training signal — the weights tier is BLOCKED**. The reflect path is a disclosed exception, served **modulate-only** (it can slow decay, never raise a level). **And the enforcement claim carries its own named bounds** — upgraded from carried-register items to surface-stated bounds at the flip, per the S11 verdict: enforcement binds on **what the examination can find**. It does not cover trust levels modulated by the direct reflect path (PA-5), sustained by stale-artifact replay (PA-10 — the safer over-trust direction, closing with the A5 recency tier), or protected by an agent navigating around the decrease surface by omission (the A2 decrease-dodge — the irreducible residual of text-based examination).

## 5. Applications — agentic AI and robotics

*(The agentic-AI positioning below is grounded in the 2026-07-07 deep-research findings; the robotics section is forward-looking application analysis by this project — the research corpus does not yet cover embodied agents, and no robotics claim here is externally validated.)*

**Agentic AI.**
- **Orchestrator→sub-agent selection.** A2A discovery today has *no quality signal at selection time* — the discernment protocol's exact slot. Fleets choosing among specialist agents can select on examined-reasoning records instead of gameable ratings (ERC-8004-style reputation is Sybil-acknowledged; marketplace ratings inherit fake-review dynamics).
- **Delegation chains with accountability.** Attenuated authority + capacity-proportional responsibility gives multi-agent systems what they currently lack: a principled answer to "who is accountable when the third agent in the chain harms a non-consenting party" — directly relevant to the EU AI Act's "the compliance boundary extends to every agent performing a high-risk function."
- **Enterprise agent governance.** The signed per-decision records are designed-in evidence for **EU AI Act Art 12 (automatic logging — live today) and Art 14 (human oversight)** — where the measured-oversight record is live now, and *demonstrable binding intervention + override* arrives with the S11 enforce flip (§6); obligations that bind **2026-08-02**; the channel law matches the measured industry consensus (policy-in-prompt cuts violations only partially; deterministic interception took a red-team CTF from 74.6% attacker success to 0%).
- **CI/deploy/ops agents.** The guard class (destructive commands, force-pushes, prod deploys) is the already-proven binding control; the trust record adds the longitudinal "should this loop get more autonomy" signal — graduated autonomy on evidence rather than vibes.

**Robotics (prospective).** The harness's action boundary is a *tool call*, and physical actuation is the ultimate irreversible tool call — the architecture transfers wherever a deliberative layer sits above a control stack:
- **Pre-actuation gating at mission level:** the H3 pattern (deterministic guard on irreversible classes) maps to task-level decisions — grasp-and-crush vs move-to-observe, entering a human-occupied zone, tool changes — where seconds-scale examination latency is tolerable. It is **not** a servo-loop or real-time controller; the fit is the deliberative tier, not the 1 kHz loop.
- **Fleet delegation:** a coordinator assigning tasks to heterogeneous robots is exactly the L1–L3 selection problem (capability fit + justice surface: which bystanders does this task touch?), with circle-scoped authority attenuation mapping naturally to workspace/zone scoping.
- **Incident forensics + certification:** signed, reproducible pre-action reasoning records are the "why-trail" regulators and insurers ask for after a physical incident; human-override supremacy (R20c) aligns with e-stop culture.
- **Honest caveats:** perception-grounded harm (a mis-classified human) is outside the extraction-trust envelope — the record attests reasoning over the *narrated* scene, not sensor truth; latency and connectivity budgets need an on-edge deployment of the deterministic Layer-2; none of this is built or claimed today.

## 6. Opportunities this opens for SageReasoning

1. **The unoccupied position (the research headline).** Every 2026 trust rail attests *identity*, *authority*, or *outcomes* — **nothing attests reasoning quality**. The Agent Passport spec concedes it in writing ("the verifier cannot detect this directly"); guardrail products check policy conformance, explicitly not ethical justification. The harness occupies: *a portable, cryptographically verifiable, per-decision credential of reasoning quality — issued against an honest, disclosed gaming ceiling.* SageReasoning's live assets already realise it (signed §4-native assessments, `examination_mode`, the accreditation GET, and now the S10 trust-record surface); the positioning sentence of record: *"a virtue-semantics judge at the action boundary, whose signed assessments are mandate-grade evidence, and whose trust record is a graded, provenance-gated, portable substrate."*
2. **From measuring to binding — the enforcing turn (the strongest new position).** Once the S11 gate is walked, the layer stops merely *measuring* reasoning quality and begins to **bind** on it: a do-not-proceed that actually holds at the action boundary, with **R20c human-override supremacy**. Every competing rail *attests* or *scores*; an enforcing, per-decision reasoning-quality gate with a live override is a materially stronger position than any of them — and the staged, qualified, honestly-bounded shape the mentor set (§4) is what lets the claim be made without over-claiming. **Conditional on the readiness review clearing and the flip working as intended.**
3. **Regulatory timing — and the shape of the evidence.** High-risk EU AI Act obligations bind 2026-08-02; NIST's agent-standards interoperability profile ships Q4 2026. The signed per-decision records are already Art 12 (automatic logging) evidence today; at enforce, Art 14 (human oversight) is satisfied not by logs alone but by **demonstrable binding intervention + override** — the strongest reading of the obligation. A shipped, honest, per-decision evidence layer ahead of both is a first-mover artifact, not a promise.
4. **The trust record as the product — now actionable.** Per ADR-012's measurement-instrument reframe, the durable value is the **per-decision character profile**; S10 makes it a readable credential and the reference harness makes `/api/reason` consumption habitual. At enforce the **graduated-autonomy signal becomes actionable**: autonomy granted (or withheld) on an enforced, decaying, unforgeable record of *how the loop reasons* — evidence, not vibes, with the grant itself gate-able.
5. **Interop leverage without interop risk.** A2A-card-extension / W3C-VC / OTel-GenAI *shapes* are already designed in (election 4). When a partner or standard asks, publication is a governance decision, not a rebuild.
6. **Complementary, not competitive, with the insurance/certification layer.** AIUC-1-style org-level certification needs per-decision evidence to audit against — a feed, not a fight.
7. **The dogfood flywheel — now turning.** S9 is live: real trust records are accumulating in the founder's own loop right now — simultaneously the instrument-fidelity validation, the first case study, the seed data S10 reads, and (via the false-hold instrument) the very measurement that will license the enforce flip.
8. **The readiness discipline itself is a credibility asset.** The layer refused to bind until a *measured* false-hold rate over the live distribution licensed it — the mentor's Q1 standard, adopted in full. That is exactly the honesty the eventual weights-tier claim will one day need, demonstrated in advance on the founder's own loop: the builder holding the assent-hinge against their own readiness to proceed.
9. **Held in reserve, deliberately:** the model-creator/weights tier (trust scores as training signal) stays **BLOCKED** until the gaming-robustness bar clears its structural residual — the discipline itself is a credibility asset when the claim is eventually made.

**The one remaining gate — the four-part readiness standard** (mentor Q1, adopted in full; all four required before the flip is re-examined): **(1)** ≥7 days of live MEASURE over a *representative* action distribution; **(2)** all four cardinal domains evaluated at least once, aggregate confidence above conservative on ≥2; **(3)** a **measured false-hold rate** on the at-action examination across the live distribution — the target: false holds on kathekon-free actions ≤ correct holds on genuinely problematic ones (this is what the false-hold instrument now makes measurable); **(4)** the G6(a) kathekon-engagement qualification encoded. Then return with the record and re-examine the assent (PR7). Until then the layer measures, records, and serves — it does not bind, except the irreversible-action guard.
