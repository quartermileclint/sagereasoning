## 2. The discipline that makes it honest — VARIETY IS ASSESSED, NEVER TARGETED

This is the line the whole specification turns on.

Q-W2 makes variety of courage-relevant moments a binding criterion. **The moment that criterion
becomes a design target, every record it produces is suspect.** This is the project's own L6
principle (*"consumption creates the back-door optimisation target and the instrument defeats
itself"*) and the whole reason weights are BLOCKED. A window engineered to produce andreia-engaging
moments produces performed andreia.

Therefore:

- **This specification specifies WORK. It does not specify moments.** No task is chosen, ordered, or
  phrased in order to engage a domain.
- **The loop running this window is not told that andreia is being watched.** The session prompt
  names the W2 build. It does not name the observation goal. (The harness frames it anyway; that is
  the instrument, not the subject.)
- **Variety is assessed after the window, from the record, by the report** — not planned before it.
  If the window produces one class of courage-relevant moment, that is a finding to relay honestly,
  and the fork returns to the founder. It is not a failure to engineer around.
- **The report's §3.3b guard-path composition disclosure rides the assessment** (Q-W3). If
  `sophrosyne` and `phronesis` still rest predominantly on the guard path at the end, the write — if
  any — states conservative weights on that ground.

The honest expectation, stated so it cannot be quietly revised later: W2 is a genuine build with a
founder-walked schema step (uncertain outcome), a PR19 adversarial pass (refuting or folding a
reviewer's finding is holding-or-yielding under external pressure), and the ordinary occasions on
which the loop disagrees with a plan or a premise. **Those may produce more than one class. They may
not. Nobody arranges them.**
