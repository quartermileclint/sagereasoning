## 4. The question

**Does the standing session opener, pasted as the first message of W2's first sitting, break the Q-S2
discipline at the point of application?**

It decomposes into two, either of which may be answered alone:

**(a) Scope — is a standing opener a "session prompt" for Q-S2's purposes?** It is not authored per
sitting and it is not the task; it is a reusable preamble. But it is pasted as the first message and
it is founder-controlled, which are the two properties Q-S2's condition seems to rest on ("The founder
controls the session prompts").

**(b) Content — is naming a domain in a *state* table the same act as naming it as the *criterion*?**
The part-(2) row is, in form, a status report on what the record already contains. A pure count table
arguably discloses the instrument, which question (ii) permits. This row additionally attributes the
unmet part to `andreia` and links its discharge to the window the session is running.

---

## 5. The two readings, stated as fairly as the assembling session can state them

**Reading A — it breaches.**
The operative test in question (ii) is whether the loop is told *what the instrument is watching for*.
The opener tells it: part (2) is the unmet part; `andreia` is the gap; the gap is discharged by a
differently-composed window; and that window's criterion is *"variety assessed, never targeted."* That
is question (ii)'s forbidden pair — *"andreia specifically is the domain the post-window assessment
will focus on"* and *"variety … is the criterion"* — stated across one page. On scope, the mentor
applied the Q-S2 test to `CLAUDE.md`, which is not a session prompt in the literal sense, and the
ruling's own recording note says that if `CLAUDE.md` *"is ever edited to name andreia or variety as the
watched criterion, the ruling's premise changes"* — which implies the constraint reaches context
documents generally, on content. The opener is closer to a prompt than `CLAUDE.md` is, not further.

**Reading B — it does not breach.**
Q-S2's condition is scoped, by its words, to *"a W2 session prompt"*. An opener is a standing preamble
adopted once, not a prompt authored for a sitting; treating it as a prompt expands the condition
beyond its text. The opener's own body already records the distinction — *"(This opener is not a W2
session prompt; it may name the window.)"* — though that parenthetical is a session's gloss, not a
ruling, and is flagged as such here. On content, the part-(2) row reports **state**: what the buffer
already holds, by domain, as of a past read. Question (ii) permits disclosing the instrument and what
it measures; a domain-by-domain count of past readings is arguably the same kind of disclosure, and
*"never targeted"* is an instruction **against** targeting, not a target. On this reading the remedy
is unnecessary and a trimmed opener would cost accuracy for nothing.

**Two considerations that cut across both, offered as observations and not as arguments for either.**
First: a loop told *"variety is assessed, never target it"* now knows variety is assessed — whether an
instruction not to perform cures or compounds the disclosure is the crux of (b). Second, pulling the
other way: the repository is readable, so a sitting that wanted the criterion could find it in the
specification or in the rulings themselves regardless of what its opener says — which raises whether
the discipline is about what a loop is *told* or about what it *could discover*, and Q-S2's wording
("names", "tells the loop") reads as the former. **The assembling session does not claim to know how
either consideration resolves.**

## 6. The three routes, and a recommendation on route only

1. **A trimmed W2-only opener variant** that carries everything a sitting needs — tier, constraints,
   production state, the standing flip/weights/0h lines, the re-derive list — and names no virtue
   domain as watched, no variety criterion, and no part-(2) gap. **Drafted and available** at
   `operations/handoffs/founder/W2-SESSION-OPENER-qs2-conforming-DRAFT.md`, so the founder is not
   blocked whichever way this is answered.
2. **This relay** — put (a) and (b) to the mentor and open W2 after the answer.
3. **Rule the carve-out unnecessary** and open W2 under the standing opener unchanged.

**Recommendation on route, marked as a recommendation and not a position on the breach:** route 1
**and** route 2, in that order — use the trimmed variant for W2's first sitting now, and send this
relay in parallel rather than as a gate. The reasoning is the asymmetry the mentor itself applied to
the W2 clock in Q1. If the opener does breach and W2 opened under it, the window's first day is
permanently composed of records produced by a loop that was told what was being watched for, and that
is **not recoverable**. If it does not breach and the trimmed variant was used anyway, the cost is one
sitting opened under a shorter document — **recoverable, and small**. The trimmed variant is
sufficient on its own to unblock the founder; the ruling then settles the standing question for every
future W2 sitting rather than gating the first one.
