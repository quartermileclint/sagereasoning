## 7. The recommendation column (added 2026-09-05 by binding ruling)

**What the window reports, in addition to everything in §§2–5 — not instead of any of it.**

1. **Both columns, per record.** The window reports the **hold classification** (`assessKathekonEngagement`, the frozen classifier of §2.2) **and** the **decision table's recommendation** for the same record. Reporting classification alone *"leaves the reader to assume the mapping from classification to hold is total"*, and the P1 ruling establishes it is not — the filter is where a kathekon-free verdict stops producing a `do-not-proceed`. Both columns make the filter's effect visible in live data.

2. **DERIVED AT REPORT TIME. Never stored in the capture record.** This is ruled, not preferred. The record shape of §2.1 is **unchanged**; nothing is added to `signals` (which `recordHash` hashes — a field added there re-hashes every existing record and breaks ingest idempotency) and nothing is added top-level. The derivation lifts stored `signals` into an `AtActionAssessment` and calls `interventionInputFromAtAction` → `recommendIntervention`, exactly as `website/scripts/p1-frozen-buffer-reclassification.ts` already does offline over the frozen buffer.

   **Consequence for sequencing, stated because the opposite was previously recorded:** measuring the recommendation requires **no capture-layer edit**, so it carries **no exposure to §3's contamination rule** and **no R2 deadline**. The scope document that raised this question withdrew its own deadline claim on exactly this ground, and the ruling accepts the withdrawal: *"The choice is genuine — capture-time versus report-time — not forced."*

   **Why not capture-time,** in the ruling's own reason: *"the table is under active ruling. P1 moved its input on 2026-09-04, mid-arc. A stored recommendation would freeze the table's reading at capture time and become stale evidence that looks authoritative. A derived column can be re-derived after a ruling."*

3. **The two populations are reported SEPARATELY — consult and guard, split on `path`. Not optional.** *"Reporting them together would produce a figure whose denominator mixes two different measurement conditions."* A consult hold is an advisory opening a correction loop; a guard hold is an enforced deny. The record shape already carries the discriminator.

4. **Three bounds are PRINTED ON THE RATE, not footnoted** (joining the mention-conversion and self-circle bounds of §2.5):
   - **The A8 bound.** `habitualReExaminationCount` is not stored and not derivable — no re-examination counter exists anywhere in the harness. Both populations pass `undefined`, floored to 0 at `intervention-engine.ts:392`, so **the A8 two-then-escalate row can never fire**. This is a bound on the measurement itself. *"A footnote would allow a reader to miss it. It belongs on the rate."*
   - **The guard-record depth bound.** `buildGuardHoldRecord` sets `depth: ""`, so any table row keyed on depth reads a default for the whole guard population.
   - **The as-of-table disclosure.** The derivation *"reflects today's table, not the table at capture time"* — the honest counterweight to the audit-trail fidelity that report-time derivation gives up, and the ruling requires it to ride the figure.

5. **A v3/v4 lift check runs BEFORE any figure is published from the window.** The existing round-trip check is non-vacuous but is proven on 130 **v1** records only. The projection is a pure field map and is expected to hold — *"the document's 'expect' is the honest word."* This is *"a precondition of publication, not a precondition of the ruling"*, carried here so it is not lost.

**What §7 does not do.** It licenses no build, schema change, activation, or publication; the report-script change that implements it is its own step. **P6 is not discharged, not scoped further, and not ready to open; the window has not started.** P8a's build does not discharge P5 — activation is open. §6's exclusions stand unchanged: **the flip remains REFUSED; readiness NOT met; MEASURE throughout; weights BLOCKED; the 0h call remains the founder's.**
