## A distinction the founder should see before weighing the readings

The sentence most often cited against the clock having started — *"the first sitting is the founder's
to open under a Q-S2-conforming prompt"* — **is not in either ruling.** It sits below
`*End of verbatim ruling.*`, under a heading its author titled *"Recording notes (the executing
session's, not the ruling's)."* A parallel note at the foot of the 05:30 file says the same thing.
**Both were written before R11 ran** (R11's first consult record is 11:48 AEST), so neither is a
statement about R11.

They may still be the right reading of the rulings. They are not themselves rulings, and the relay
should not present them as such.

**And the weight that survives that observation, stated so this section does not function as an
argument for one side** (a blind reviewer found the memo's version of this passage did exactly that):
a recording note written by the session that had just captured the ruling, with no stake in R11, is
the best contemporaneous evidence of the ruling's plain sense; **two such notes say the same thing
independently**, hours apart, from different source rulings; "written before R11" means neither note
judged R11 **and** equally that neither was written to exclude it — a general statement is not
disabled as to a later instance by predating it; and this project routinely treats executing sessions'
recording notes as load-bearing (the 05:30 file's own note corrects a factual error *inside* the
ruling it accompanies, and the eight-question ruling relies on that correction).
