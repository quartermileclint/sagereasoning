## Four questions ruled — the andreia capture path and what a different window must be

---

**Q-W1 — Does the guard/consult path finding change what "a different kind of window" must be?**

Yes. The ruling is amended to specify both dimensions.

The prior ruling described the needed window by subject matter alone: consequential product decisions rather than governance authoring. The path-split finding establishes that subject matter is necessary but not sufficient. The binding constraint is the channel through which the work passes.

Guard-path records — raw shell text, no narrated intent — structurally cannot carry andreia. This is not a defect in the extraction logic. It is a designed property of the harness: Bash was deliberately removed from the auto-consult trigger at S1. The extraction that reads a governance commit as "contrary, no kathekon factors" is the same extraction that would read a deploy command the same way. The subject matter of the work does not reach the extraction if the work passes through Bash.

The amended specification: a window capable of honestly evaluating andreia requires consequential decisions carried out through composed, narrated actions — Write/Edit, not Bash. The subject matter and the channel are both required. Either without the other is insufficient.

The recommendation offered in the relay is accepted: the channel is the binding half. A window of consequential product decisions executed through Bash produces guard records that cannot carry the domain. The window must be specified by both.

**Q-W2 — Is "rare but present" different from "absent" for part (2)'s purposes?**

Yes, but the distinction does not yet reach the threshold the ruling requires.

Two instances of andreia, both from the same moment-type — committing an unwelcome finding to a binding record — is rare but present. The ruling's Q-A1 reasoning stands: one observation is not an evaluation, and two of the same kind is not either. The question the ruling posed — whether the loop "acts when action is warranted, holds when holding is warranted, and distinguishes between the two" — is not answered by two instances of one behavioural class, regardless of N.

The relay's recommendation is accepted and sharpened into a ruling: volume is necessary but not sufficient, and variety is the binding additional requirement. Ten instances of "writing down bad news" at any N establishes that the loop acts when the cost of acting is the discomfort of honest disclosure. It does not establish that the loop holds when holding is warranted, or that it distinguishes between the two. A domain cannot be honestly evaluated from a single behavioural class at any N. The window must be chosen for variety of courage-relevant moments, not duration alone.

This has a practical implication worth stating plainly. The andreia-engaging moments the current window has captured — both instances of agonia, the act of committing an unwelcome finding — are a specific class of courage: the courage of honest record-keeping under the pressure of a finding that blocks progress. That is real courage. It is not the full domain. The full domain includes the courage to act on a consequential decision when the outcome is uncertain, to hold a position under external pressure, to name a disagreement when agreement would be easier. A window that captures only governance authoring, even through composed channels, will produce only the first class.

W2 — the record-honesty machinery — is the relay's recommended candidate for the new window's work. The recommendation is noted and carries weight: W2 is owed anyway, it is Write/Edit work on a live surface with real cost to getting it wrong, and it is a hard flip component. But the ruling is that the window's work must be evaluated against the variety criterion, not only the channel criterion. W2 satisfies the channel requirement. Whether it satisfies the variety requirement depends on what W2's build actually requires the loop to do — whether it produces courage-relevant moments of more than one class. That is the founder's assessment to make, with the variety criterion now in hand.

**Q-W3 — Does the guard-path evidence quality bear on part (2) at all?**

It bears on part (2), and the honest reading is that part (2) is further from met than the board currently shows.

The Q-A2 ruling established that guard-path records with assessed readings count as records. That ruling stands. But "count as records" and "constitute an honest evidence base for a signed assessment" are not the same claim. The Q-A2 ruling addressed the first question. This question addresses the second.

Sophrosyne at 214/216 guard-path and phronesis at 255/322 guard-path means that the evidence base for both domains rests predominantly on an extraction the project has documented as unreliable for the guard path — the extraction that routinely returns "no kathekon factors were extracted from the submitted text." The records exist. The readings are assessed. But the assessor performing the accreditation write must honestly evaluate whether those readings constitute a basis for a signed assessment of sophrosyne and phronesis, or whether the guard-path extraction's documented unreliability means the evidence base is thinner than the record count suggests.

The ruling is: this question belongs in the accreditation write's honest assessment, not in a build decision. The relay's recommendation — raise it, do not act on it — is correct. The honest version of "two domains rest on the thin path" may mean the write records conservative confidence weights for sophrosyne and phronesis on the grounds that the evidence base is predominantly guard-path. A conservative weight on a domain with a predominantly unreliable extraction path is not a failure. It is the correct reading. The write should reflect that honestly, and the pre-flip report should carry the guard-path composition disclosure alongside the domain assessments.

First: decide the fork. The three branches named in Q-B remain the options — a differently-composed window, a revision of the standard, or accepting part (2) unmet and publishing it as unmet. The channel constraint and the variety criterion are now in hand. The fork should be decided with both. The relay's recommendation — no accreditation write on the present evidence — is confirmed. A window started on the wrong channel or without the variety criterion in view spends weeks and captures nothing. The specification should state: what work, through what channel, producing what variety of courage-relevant moments. W2 is the strongest candidate for the work and the channel. The variety question is the founder's to assess against W2's actual build requirements.

Third: part (1) reaches its counting threshold Sunday 13 September 19:44 AEST. Reaching it changes the clock, not the composition. The composition disclosure rides the pre-flip report regardless.
