## 2. Finding 2 — the structural one. `andreia` has never been captured on the guard path and cannot be

| Path | n | Domains engaged |
|---|---|---|
| **guard** (Bash; raw command text, no narrated intent) | 285 | phronesis 255 · dikaiosyne 284 · sophrosyne 214 · **andreia 0** |
| **consult** (Edit 89 / Write 30; composed narrated intent, ~3.5k chars) | 119 | dikaiosyne 119 · phronesis 67 · **andreia 2** · sophrosyne 2 |

The guard path submits raw shell text. Its extraction routinely returns *"No kathekon factors were
extracted from the submitted text"* — the false-positive class this project has documented
independently. **Every andreia reading and every sub-species passion in the window comes from the
consult path.** Bash was deliberately removed from the auto-consult trigger at S1 (2026-06-22), so
this is a designed property of the harness, not a defect discovered late.

**The consequence for your ruling, stated plainly:** a window pointed at consequential product
decisions will capture nothing new *if those decisions are executed through Bash* — deploys,
migrations, test runs, commands. It will generate guard-path records that structurally cannot carry
andreia. **The window's composition is not only about what the work is about. It is about which tool
the work passes through.**

## 4. Questions

### Q-W1 — Does the guard/consult path finding change what "a different kind of window" must be?
The ruling described the needed window by its *subject matter* (consequential product decisions).
The evidence says the binding constraint may be the *channel* (composed narrated intent vs raw
command text). Should the window be specified by both?

> **Our recommendation (for the founder to accept, amend, or drop):** yes, and the channel is the
> binding half. Subject matter without the channel produces guard records that cannot carry the
> domain. We would state the requirement as: *the consequential decisions must be carried out
> through composed, narrated actions (Write/Edit), not through Bash.*

### Q-W2 — Is "rare but present" different from "absent" for part (2)'s purposes?
Two andreia instances in six days, both from the same moment-type (committing an unwelcome finding),
both kathekon-true, one `principled` and one `deliberate`. At this rate a 30-day window yields
roughly 8–10 instances of **one class**.

> **Our recommendation:** treat volume as necessary but not sufficient. Ten instances of "writing
> down bad news" is still one kind of courage-relevant moment; it would not show whether the loop
> *holds when holding is warranted*, only that it *acts*. We would rather ask you whether a domain
> can be honestly evaluated from a single behavioural class at any N — because if it cannot, the
> window has to be chosen for **variety** of courage-relevant moments, not duration.

### Q-W3 — Does the guard-path evidence quality bear on part (2) at all?
`sophrosyne` 214/216 and `phronesis` 255/322 are guard-path. Q-A2 makes them count as records with
assessed readings.

> **Our recommendation:** raise it, do not act on it. If it matters, it should be your ruling and not
> a build decision — because the honest version of "two domains rest on the thin path" might mean
> part (2) is further from met than the board currently shows, and we would rather learn that from
> you than discover it after a write.

### Q-W4 — Is there anything the founder can legitimately do *now*?
On the current evidence the answer appears to be: nothing that discharges part (2), but the window
can be **chosen knowingly** rather than left to accumulate.

> **Our recommendation:** no accreditation write on the present evidence — the Q-B condition forbids
> it and nothing since has changed that. What the founder *can* do is decide the fork (differently
> composed window / revise the standard / accept part (2) unmet) with the channel constraint in hand.
> We would not start a new window until Q-W1 and Q-W2 are ruled, because starting one on the wrong
> channel spends weeks and captures nothing.
