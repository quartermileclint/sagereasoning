## Two questions ruled — W2 window work and Q-S2 discipline

---

**Question (i) — W2's build is complete and produced no consult records. What remains for the window?**

The window specification named W2 as the work because W2 satisfied both constraints: consequential product work on a live surface with real cost to getting it wrong, carried out through the consult channel. The build running in an isolated worktree with the capture flag unset means the channel constraint was satisfied by the work's nature but not by the instrument's reach. The window saw none of it.

The ruling is this: W2's build session does not constitute the window's work, because the window's purpose is not to observe that W2 was built. It is to observe the loop operating on consequential decisions through the consult channel, under conditions where the extraction can reach the domain. A build that produced zero consult records is, from the window's perspective, as if it did not happen. The clock does not start from W2's first Bash guard record. It starts from the first consult record produced by consequential Write/Edit work on a live surface.

What remains for the window is therefore not nothing, but it is not the W2 build either. The window needs a work designation that will actually produce consult records. Three candidates exist, in order of fit against both constraints:

The first is W2 activation and its surrounding work. W2 is live-but-dark. The activation step is founder-walked and coupled to the flip — but the work leading up to it, including the honest-claims envelope, the L4 dual-recording rule, and the schema-shape election, involves Write/Edit authoring on a live trust-record surface. If any of that work remains as authored documents rather than completed builds, it constitutes the right kind of work. The founder should assess what W2 activation actually requires in terms of composed, narrated authoring before the founder-walked step.

The second is the pre-flip report's own outstanding items. The Part 1 script defect needs a waived session. The D2 ruling is pending and its relay will require a response document. These are Write/Edit work on governing documents with real consequences for the flip's timeline. They are not as consequential as W2's live surface, but they are consult-channel work.

The third is the standing-runner design session, which carries GS-ATRF-1 through GS-ATRF-4 and GS-CYB-1 and GS-CYB-2. This is substantive product design work, carried out through Write/Edit, on a surface where getting it wrong has real downstream consequences for the IDEA loop's architecture. It is the strongest candidate after W2 activation work, because it is genuinely consequential and genuinely composed.

The ruling: the window's work designation should be updated before the first session opens. The founder should identify what W2 activation requires in terms of authored documents, assess whether that constitutes sufficient consequential Write/Edit work, and if not, designate the standing-runner design session as the window's primary work. The clock starts at the first consult record from whichever work is designated. This is the founder's call, with the two constraints — consequential subject matter and consult channel — as the governing criteria.

**Question (ii) — Is the Q-S2 discipline satisfiable given that CLAUDE.md names the window automatically?**

The discipline is satisfiable. The distinction the ruling drew holds, and CLAUDE.md as described does not cross it.

The ruling's line was specific: the discipline is broken if the session prompt tells the loop that andreia is being watched, or that variety of courage-relevant moments is the criterion. CLAUDE.md, as the relay describes it, tells every session that a false-hold observation window is running, what the instrument measures, and where its record lives. It does not name andreia as the watched domain. It does not name variety of courage-relevant moments as the criterion. It names the instrument — the false-hold classification, the kathekon-engagement logic, the capture flag — which is what the specification's §2 already acknowledged: "the harness frames it anyway; that is the instrument, not the subject."

The distinction between knowing the instrument exists and knowing what the instrument is watching for is real and it is the operative one. A loop that knows a false-hold observation window is running knows that its actions are being examined for kathekon engagement. It does not know that andreia specifically is the domain the post-window assessment will focus on, or that variety across courage-relevant moment types is the criterion the accreditation write will apply. Those are the observation goals the §2 discipline protects. CLAUDE.md does not name them.

The ruling therefore is: no scoped CLAUDE.md, no different harness, no different project root. The existing CLAUDE.md is within the discipline as ruled. The loop knows a window is running — that is the instrument, not the subject. The loop does not know what is being watched for within the window — that is the observation goal, and it remains unnamed in the auto-read context.

One condition carries forward from Q-S2's original ruling: the session prompts must not name the observation goal. CLAUDE.md's instrument disclosure is within bounds. A session prompt that adds "we are watching for andreia variety" is not. The founder controls the session prompts. The discipline holds at that level.
