## 2. The finding — stated as evidence, not as a claim on the ruling

**The standing session opener** (`STANDING-SESSION-OPENER-grounded-foundations.md`, Version 2026-09-13,
founder-adopted) **is pasted as the first message of every session.** Preparing W2's first sitting
surfaced that it names both things Q-S2's condition names.

**Q-S2's condition, verbatim:**

> "If a W2 session prompt names the observation goal — if it tells the loop that andreia is being
> watched, or that variety of courage-relevant moments is the criterion — the discipline is broken at
> the point of application, regardless of what the specification says."

**The 2026-09-13 ruling, question (ii), passing `CLAUDE.md` on that test:**

> "It does not name andreia as the watched domain. It does not name variety of courage-relevant
> moments as the criterion."

…and stating the operative distinction:

> "The distinction between knowing the instrument exists and knowing what the instrument is watching
> for is real and it is the operative one. … It does not know that andreia specifically is the domain
> the post-window assessment will focus on, or that variety across courage-relevant moment types is
> the criterion the accreditation write will apply. Those are the observation goals the §2 discipline
> protects."

**What the opener contains** (grepped first-hand; line numbers as at this writing, the quoted text is
the anchor):

| Where | Text |
|---|---|
| **body, line 298** — the readiness board's part-(2) row | *"**andreia 0 / 4** (all consult…)"*; *"Every andreia reading is on the consult path; the guard path has never carried one"*; **and in the same row** part (2) *"is discharged, if at all, by a **differently-composed window** — now specified, approved and ruled (below)"* |

| **header lines 19–24 and addendum §H** | both quote Q-S2's trigger phrases in full **while explaining the collision** |

**Two observations the session offers as evidence:**

**(a) The carve-out warning is itself a breach.** The block explaining why the part-(2) row is a
problem re-states both triggers. **So a reading instruction — "skip the part-(2) row" — cannot remedy
the document.** Whatever is ruled, the remedy is a separate document or nothing.

**(b) The session's own remedy reproduced the defect, twice, in different forms.** The first draft of
the conforming opener carried its omissions table in-document — **quoting the forbidden criterion
verbatim while explaining that the document omits it**; a grep caught it and it was split into a

disclosive: it told a sitting that material was deliberately withheld and that a document discussing
it existed, without naming its object. **That pointer was deleted.** Recorded because it may bear on
the ruling: **an explanation of an omission tends to reconstitute it**, which is a property of the
remedy class, not of this document.

**The session's own reading, offered as a reading and not as a claim on the ruling:** the
state-versus-criterion distinction is real — a bare four-domain count table would be a genuinely hard

## 3. Questions for ruling

### ⚖️ Q-A1 — Does Q-S2 reach a standing session opener?

Q-S2's condition is scoped by its words to *"a W2 session prompt"*. A standing opener is **not
authored per sitting** — it is a reusable preamble, adopted once — but it **is pasted as the sitting's
first message** and it **is founder-controlled**, which are the two properties the condition's closing
sentences rest on (*"The founder controls the session prompts"*).

**Evidence pulling toward "yes, it reaches it":** the 2026-09-13 ruling applied the Q-S2 test to
`CLAUDE.md` — which is plainly not a session prompt in the literal sense — and passed it **on
content**; and its recording note states that *"If `CLAUDE.md` is ever edited to name andreia or
variety as the watched criterion, the ruling's premise changes."*

**Evidence pulling toward "no":** the condition's own wording, and the fact that the opener's body
already carries a session's gloss to that effect (*"(This opener is not a W2 session prompt; it may
name the window.)"* — **a recording note, not a ruling**, and flagged as such).

### ⚖️ Q-A2 — Is naming a domain in a *state* table the same act as naming the *criterion*?

The part-(2) row is, in form, a **status report**: counts already in the record, as of a past read.
Question (ii) permits disclosing the instrument and what it measures.

**Two sub-parts, and the second is where the session thinks the answer lies:**
1. Would a **bare** per-domain count table — four domains, counts only, no link to the window's
   purpose — be permitted instrument disclosure, or already a breach?
2. Does the answer change when the row **also** identifies the unmet readiness part, attributes it to
   one domain, and states that the part is discharged by *"a differently-composed window"* — the
   window the sitting is running?

### ⚖️ Q-A3 — Does the discipline govern what a sitting is TOLD, or what it COULD DISCOVER?

The repository is readable by any sitting. Nothing prevents one from opening the window
specification, the standing opener, or the rulings themselves and reading the criterion there. **A
trimmed opener controls what a sitting is told, not what it could find.**

Q-S2's verbs — *"names"*, *"tells the loop"* — read as governing the former. **But the answer
determines whether a trimmed opener is a genuine remedy or a cosmetic one**, and therefore whether
anything further (scoped context, a different project root — both refused for `CLAUDE.md` in the
2026-09-13 ruling) would be needed. **The session takes no position and notes the ruling already
refused those remedies for a different document.**

### ⚖️ Q-B1 — May the record-level compliance-not-virtue clause be applied while the field it describes cannot appear?

*(The founder may regard this as theirs — it is an R18 wording decision. It is put here because it
turns on L7's own obligation and on the honest-claims discipline rather than on wording taste.)*

**The facts.** Mentor L7 requires the clause *"at the record level **and** travel with
enforcement-class entries inline."* **The inline half is built, merged and battery-locked.** The
record-level half is staged and unapplied. But `enforcement_outcomes` is **structurally absent from
every served payload** while `SUBSTRATE_ENFORCEMENT_RECORD_ENABLED` is unset — which it is, everywhere
— while `TRUST_RECORD_ENVELOPE` is served on **every** trust-record read. So applying the staged
wording today publishes, on a live public surface, an item whose first sentence reads *"Entries in
`enforcement_outcomes` describe outcomes the infrastructure produced…"* — describing entries that
cannot appear.

**Against applying now:** ADR-013 §8's own amendment history states the discipline twice — *"a
present-tense claim would publish behaviour that does not exist — the precise defect class this
amendment corrects"* (2026-08-25) and *"so the record cannot simultaneously display a mechanism and
disclaim that it exists"* (2026-08-30) — and states the ordering rule **four times**: *"The three R18
public surfaces carry the amendment **after** this code edit, never ahead of it."*

**For applying now:** the sentence is **definitional, not existential** — it says what such entries
describe, in the manner of a schema note. And **L7 obliges** the record-level clause; deferring it is
not a neutral act while the inline half already ships.

**The question:** does the honest-claims discipline require the record-level clause to wait for
activation, or does L7's obligation bind now — and if it binds now, should the trigger clause be
recast in the future tense on the 2026-08-25 precedent rather than the state being asserted?

## 4. Recommendations — marked as recommendations, and separated from the questions

**On route (Q-A1–A3), and this is a recommendation on sequencing only, not on the ruling:**
**use the conforming opener for W2's first sitting now, and relay these questions in parallel rather
than as a gate.** The reasoning is the asymmetry the mentor itself applied to the W2 clock in Q1: if
the opener does breach and W2 opened under it, the window's first day is permanently composed of
records produced by a loop that was told what was being watched for — **not recoverable**. If it does
not breach and a shorter document was used anyway, the cost is one sitting opened under a trimmed
preamble — **recoverable, and small**. **A conforming draft exists and is grep-verified clean**
(`operations/handoffs/founder/W2-SESSION-OPENER-qs2-conforming-DRAFT.md`), so the founder is not
blocked on the answer; the ruling then settles the standing question for every future W2 sitting
rather than gating the first one.

to activation.** Two structural recommendations that hold whichever is ruled:
1. **Decide the envelope and the three R18 doc surfaces on the same reasoning.** The staged file is
   internally inconsistent: it defers the doc surfaces *"once the field can actually appear"* and
   applies no such test to the envelope, in the same document.
2. **Substitute L7's verbatim closing sentences for the staged paraphrase** — the staged file's own
   recommendation, and the exact text was verified this session: *"The record shows what examinations
