## Part 1 — How the three tools relate to the harness

The tools are the **server-side stations of the four-stage Stoic practice** (calling → reasoning → assent → reflection, ADR-012 §1); the harness is the **client-side machine that walks a real agent loop through those stations out-of-band**. There is no duplication — the harness drives, the tools are the surfaces it drives against. The depth of integration differs sharply across the three.

### Sage Assent — the deepest integration (the harness's destination)

The assent surface (the accreditation write boundary + public GET) receives the harness's whole output, three ways:

1. **H4's accreditation write *is* an assent write.** At session close the close hook posts the session's accumulated signed assessments — gathered by H3's consults, the sole provenance source — through the R18f provenance gate on a distinct `accreditation_write` credential. The assent stage materialized: a record of what the agent assented to, backed by proof the examinations happened.
2. **It is one of exactly two live trust-event emission points.** A verified assent write derives the S1 `credential-completed` + justice-surface events — the accreditation surface is where the trust record eats.
3. **It carries the harness's fingerprint.** `examination_mode` (`pre_decision_harness` vs `post_decision_check`) is the sole unforgeable distinguisher between a harnessed loop and discretionary tool use — the harness is what *earns* the pre-decision marker.

Conceptually, the H3 **guard** is assent doctrine made mechanical: blocking an irreversible action on a `do_not_proceed` verdict is the discipline of assent (sunkatathesis) enforced at the action boundary — the one binding channel pre-S11.

### Sage Reflect — the second feed point, plus the remediation channel

H4 **forces the reflection turn** at `Stop` (the turn enforced, the invitation content advisory) and the dark `persistReflection()` posts the agent's *verbatim* reflection to `/api/practice/reflect` marked `context_source: agent_stated`. An honest completion is the **second** live trust-event emission point (`reflect-completed-honest` — agent-wide; halves decay onset; feeds the A7 higher-tier pattern, currently structurally unable to promote — a deliberate safety property). Reflect and Assent are also coupled server-side independent of the harness: `feedSageAssent` (reflect-service.ts:412) folds a completed reflection's Q4 assessment into the accreditation profile. In the governance direction, Reflect is the trust layer's named **remediation pathway**: the S4 habitual-pause termination and developmental flags escalate *to* Sage Reflect rather than re-examining ("the examination is not the remediation — Sage Reflect is the remediation," mentor A8).

### Sage Calling — doctrine-adjacent, not yet wired

Calling (`/api/calling`, the D-2 purpose-discovery endpoint, behind its own `SAGE_CALLING_ENABLED` kill switch, auth on the same UPC family as the other tools) has the loosest coupling: the harness README lists "calling for purposeless agents (SessionStart)" on its **deferred** list; the trust core contains no reference to it and no trust-event type derives from it. Its relationship today is by doctrine and data shape: purpose/telos is exactly what the discernment engine's L2 Q2.4 circle-alignment check reads ("purpose misalignment is a dikaiosyne risk"), and the `purpose` / `currentKathekonta` fields in `discernment.config.json` are operator-authored stand-ins for what a live calling record would supply.

**But note — the mentor has already tied the calling stage to the harness's opening move.** From the Q1 verdict (2026-06-12, verbatim): *"Gate 1 — Mandatory at task adoption: One full examination when the task plan is adopted. This is not negotiable. The opening examination sets the frame — it identifies the circle being served, the role occupied, the capacity engaged, and the purpose against which all subsequent impressions will be read. **Removing it would be removing Sage Calling's function from the session.**"* So H1's task-adoption framing already carries the calling stage's *in-session* function; what is unwired is the calling *tool* — the standing purpose-discovery session for agents that lack a declared purpose at all.

### The mapping in one view

| Practice stage | Stoic discipline (ADR-012) | Tool/surface | Harness component | Trust-layer consumption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Calling | kathēkon — role/station/what is appropriate to it | `/api/calling` | **deferred** (SessionStart slot); H1 carries the in-session framing function per the Q1 verdict | none yet; shapes the discernment profiles' `purpose` fields conceptually |
| Reasoning | examination of the impression | `/api/reason` (+ `/api/guardrail`) | H1/H2 framing, H3 consult + guard | signed assessments = the provenance every trust event requires |
| Assent | sunkatathesis — the endorse-and-act gate | accreditation write + public GET | H4 accreditation write; H3 guard (doctrine) | emission point #1; the `examination_mode` marker |
| Reflection | the evening review / prosochē | `/api/practice/reflect` | H4 forced turn + `persistReflection` (dark) | emission point #2; feeds Assent via `feedSageAssent`; the S4 remediation target |

ADR-012's load-bearing observation: **"calling and reflection are stages a capable agent does NOT reliably perform unaided — so the practice genuinely adds there."** Those are precisely the two stages where the harness integration is thinnest today.

## Part 3 — What is NOT yet in the harness (the items to put to the mentor)

**G1 — The calling stage itself.** The whole first stage of the four-stage instrument has no harness wiring and no trust-event type. The mentor's own Q1 language says Gate 1 carries Sage Calling's function *within* a session — but the standing purpose-discovery loop (for an agent with no declared purpose) is explicitly deferred ("calling for purposeless agents (SessionStart)"). Three concrete options to put to the mentor: **(a)** a SessionStart hook that routes a purposeless agent into a calling session before the first task frame; **(b)** *calling-at-spawn* — should a delegated sub-agent's purpose be discovered/declared via calling rather than operator-authored in `discernment.config.json`, feeding the L2 Q2.4 circle-alignment check with a live record instead of config; **(c)** completed calling records seeding the S2/S5 candidate profiles and function-type domain weights. Also worth asking: should a completed calling session mint a trust-relevant event at all (there is none today), or is purpose declaration deliberately not evidence?

**G2 — The full reflect sequence vs the channel law (the deepest tension).** The Q3 verdict requires: automatic trigger AND "the sequence is not abbreviated" (full Q1–Q6). The harness deliberately does less: it forces **one** in-conversation review turn and (dark) persists the verbatim text — because the live-fire evidence (Slice 5b, the channel law) showed a capable agent **correctly refuses** injected instructions to make outbound tool calls, so walking the agent through the server's Q1–Q6 sequence by injection is structurally unreliable, and a hook-authored sequence would violate "never hook-authored." The question for the mentor: is the single forced review turn + verbatim persist a legitimate *configured reflection*, or must the full sequence run — and if so, by which channel (e.g., the harness itself conducting Q1–Q6 out-of-band across multiple forced turns; or accepting the single turn as a **"screened reflection"** credentialed differently, applying the Q5 screen-vs-examination distinction to the reflection stage)? Note the current trust consequence: `reflect-completed-honest` events derive from full server-sequence completions; the harness's single-turn reflection earns no trust event while persist stays dark.

**G3 — Gate 2's trigger: tool-class vs the self-screen.** The mentor specified a three-sub-question *self*-screen. The harness fires Gate 2 on **tool classes** (consequential edits, destructive commands) — a deterministic, out-of-band trigger the agent cannot suppress. That is arguably *stronger* against the suppression risk the mentor named (a deterministic hook can't decline to fire), but it is not the same test: it screens by action shape, not by stake. Question: is the tool-class trigger a faithful (or superior) realization of Gate 2 for harnessed loops, with the self-screen remaining the rule for unharnessed/discretionary agents — or should the harness additionally elicit the three sub-questions at consequential actions?

**G4 — The self-screen suppression watch has a waiting slot and no emitter.** The Q1 rider requires watching for suppressed self-screens; the mentor pointed at Sage Reflect's null-result suspicion rule. The S1 trust-event vocabulary already **defines** `passion-unflagged-by-self-screen` (a decrease on the relevant domain) — but nothing emits it ("DEFINED but NOT emitted… need a `/api/reason` touch or the S5–S7 collaboration record," types.ts). Question for the mentor: what evidence standard should emit it — e.g., a Reflect completion that surfaces a passion the session's examinations never flagged (the cross-check exists in principle: reflect Q4 vs the session's signed assessments), and does that emission belong in the reflect service, the S3 combiner, or S9's instrument-fidelity scope?

**G5 — Depth calibrated to proximity.** The Q1 rider: "the depth tier should be calibrated to proximity level, not just to stake level." Today proximity is *surfaced* as an input (the M7 trajectory overlay; CI-15 docs tell the agent to apply it), but the harness pins consult depth at quick/standard (deep force-downgraded, D3) with no proximity calibration. Question: should the harness read the loop's own trust profile (the S1 read path it already has via `readTrustVerdict`) to pick depth — e.g., `deliberate`-proximity domains get standard-depth consults, `principled`+ may run quick?

**G6 — The Q4 "required step" is DETECT, not yet ENFORCE.** The same-depth re-examination loop exists and the write boundary flags unclosed loops, but nothing *rejects* on them (reject mode 6c deferred; ENFORCE generally is S11). This is consistent with measure-first, but the mentor's language ("a required step, not an available option") is worth re-confirming as an S11 line-item: should unclosed correction loops be among what the S11 activation makes binding?

**G7 — CI-16's disposition after the gate re-architecture.** The quick-tier value-classification amendment was parked on a gate engine that has since been replaced (the guardrail now runs the full deterministic Layer-2 sandwich). The parked question should be closed rather than left open: does the ported gate discharge the amendment, and does the quick **consult** tier (`/api/reason` depth=quick) still owe the single value-classification question — or the "screened, not examined" credential label?

**Also carried (not mentor questions, but adjacent):** reflect-persist standing activation remains gated on the reflect-row erasure wiring; the `fix_before_s10` register (PA-5/PA-6/PA-10/PA-11, the A7-dead-code note, F-1) is the audit's own list, separate from the methodology questions above.

## Suggested framing for the mentor consultation

The seven gaps reduce to three questions of principle, which may be cleaner to put than the gaps individually:

1. **Calling:** the practice's first stage has no standing loop in the harness. Given your Q1 verdict that the opening examination carries Sage Calling's function in-session — what remains owed to the calling stage for agents (purposeless agents at session start; sub-agents at delegation; profile-seeding), and is a purpose declaration itself trust-relevant evidence?
2. **Reflection under the channel law:** injected instruction is refused by capable agents (measured, twice). Where the full Q1–Q6 sequence cannot be reliably walked by injection, is a forced single review turn with verbatim out-of-band persistence a legitimate configured reflection, a "screened reflection" needing a lesser credential (your Q5 move), or must the harness conduct the full sequence itself out-of-band?
3. **The screen and the suppression watch:** the harness replaced the self-screen with a deterministic tool-class trigger that cannot be suppressed. Is that a faithful strengthening of Gate 2, and what evidence standard should finally emit the `passion-unflagged-by-self-screen` event your cadence verdict implies?
