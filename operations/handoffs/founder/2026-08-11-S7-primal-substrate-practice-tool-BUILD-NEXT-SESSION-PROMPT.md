## 3. The exercise

**Entry point: the trait** — which primal impulse is most active for the practitioner right now.

**Then `DIAGNOSTIC_SEQUENCE` as five questions**, with the trait narrowing step 4's candidates:

1. **The specific impression** — required. Heading 4's specificity requirement lands here, and the
   field's own prompt should carry the mentor's example: not *"I felt competitive"* but *"I felt
   competitive when X said Y, because I interpreted it as a threat to Z."*
2. The **false belief** that drove the assent.
3. **Did the impulse exceed what reason warranted?**
4. **Which sub-species was operative** — practitioner-selected from the local vocabulary, narrowed by
   the trait.
5. **The correct judgement that would replace the false one.**

**The four v1 pathways** (C13), three landing on committed sub-species IDs and one not:

| Trait (cite by name) | Maps to | Committed ID |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competition · Hierarchy/Dominance | philodoxia — love of honour | `:323` |
| Resource Acquisition / Foraging Optimization | philoplousia — love of wealth | `:322` |
| Threat Avoidance / Self-Preservation | phobos sub-species | `agonia :348` · `oknos :344` · `aischyne :345` |
| Reciprocity / Conditional Cooperation | **not a passion sub-species** — the `praxis` failure mode | `:588` |

**The fourth mode is structurally different and must not be forced into the sub-species shape** —
doing so would either invent a sub-species (R7 source-fidelity violation) or drop the pathway. Its
mentor-supplied question set, verbatim:

> *"is this cooperation grounded in recognition of the other as a rational being, or in expected
> return? What would the action look like if the expected return were removed?"*

The second is a **counterfactual, not a diagnosis** — it has no analogue in the other three, which is
why the mode genuinely differs. Its corpus anchor is `praxis`: *"externally correct behaviour driven by
wrong reasons."*

**The framing the mentor requires, in the page's own copy:** noticing the impulse *"is not failing —
they are generating examination material."* That is S1's reframe made visible at the point of use.

**The gate: exactly one, classification-only** — on **step 1's specificity** (`specific | general`),
because specificity is what Heading 4 names as *"the evidence of genuine examination rather than
formulaic self-report."* Deterministic pre-authored messages, no LLM-authored commentary, fail-open,
cached, `MODEL_FAST`. **It must never touch steps 2–5** — in particular it must never classify, score,
or grade the **correct judgement** (step 5); that would make the tool an assessor of the practitioner's
philosophy, which no sibling does. **Pin it, and mutation-verify the pin** — the `/sage-compass` lesson
is that a pin checking only a *parameter name* is defeated by a rename-and-pass-positionally.
