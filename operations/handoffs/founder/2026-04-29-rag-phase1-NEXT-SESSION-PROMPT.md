The architecture under design must (a) understand the founder's raw inputs and practitioner / project context, (b) retrieve the most relevant Stoic Brain passages given that input plus memory, (c) compose a reply in the LLM's voice that paraphrases the retrieved passages without inventing advice not present in them, and (d) verify the reply traces back to the retrieved passages.
