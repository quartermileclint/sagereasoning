What is missing is **the sage as a deliberate imaginative tool** — the Stoic practice of asking, in a specific situation, *what would the sage do?* And the mentor is emphatic that this is **not** a vague aspiration but a **structured** exercise, and that its output is **not a verdict**.

## Three genuine design forks to settle at open (recommendations given; decide, don't survey)

1. **The gate — and it is genuinely constrained on both sides.** The mentor says two things that pull opposite ways: *"the distance is **not a verdict**"* (so **nothing may score, rank, or grade the distance** — that would convert the compass into a judgement) **and** *"this is **not a vague aspiration**"* (which is exactly the concreteness worry #8's `prepared`/`vague` gate was built for). **Recommendation:** a **light, classification-only gate on the *complete expression* field ONLY** (`concrete` / `vague` — is the sage's expression situation-anchored, or a platitude like "act with integrity"?), rendered in the deterministic-message + revise-in-place style of `/morning` and `/view-from-above`; fail-open, cached, `MODEL_FAST`; the LLM authors **no** stored commentary. **The distance field must NEVER be classified, scored, or ranked** — it is practitioner-authored developmental orientation. If that split feels too fine, the honest fallback is **gate-free** (the `/oikeiosis` circle-extension precedent). Either is defensible; scoring the distance is not.
2. **The virtue vocabulary + the distance — THE BOUNDARY TRAP.** The obvious move is to reuse the engine's four cardinal domains and the `katorthoma_proximity` scale (`reflexive → habitual → deliberate → principled → sage_like`). **Do not import them.** `sage-reflect`'s `proximity-domains.ts` (the KP-04 `weakest()` module) and `stoic-brain` are **forbidden modules** on the boundary battery — importing either breaks measurement-neutrality and fails the gate. **Recommendation:** define a plain-language virtue vocabulary **locally** in the route/page (the exact pattern the `/oikeiosis` circle-extension used for its five circles — a local `const`, no engine import), and make **the distance practitioner-authored** (free text, optionally with a self-selected coarse reading like *far / some way / close*) — **never** the engine's proximity ranks and **never** tool-computed. This keeps the compass a bearing, not a score, and keeps the file boundary clean.
3. **The passion-diagnosis complement — prose, not coupling.** The mentor frames the compass and the passion diagnosis as bracketing the action from both sides. There is a live `/passion-log` (`passion_events`). **Recommendation: render the complementarity as page prose only** — no code coupling, no FK to `passion_events` (this is exactly the **#8 lesson**, where "morning declares the intention, evening assesses whether it held" was kept as page prose and never wired to the reflect engine). A future linkage can be its own decision.
