The mentor's survey drew a sharp line the oikeiosis surface currently sits on the wrong side of: *"The current tools diagnose where the practitioner is reasoning from. They do not yet provide the exercise that builds the capacity to reason from a wider circle."* The live `/oikeiosis` page is a **quarterly diagnostic** (which stage's actions you took; a philodoxia flag). The mentor names the missing half — the **active extension practice**: *"the Stoic exercise of deliberately drawing the outer circles inward, treating distant others as closer than they feel… bring the fourth circle — all rational beings — into the same felt proximity as the second circle — family and intimates."* This session builds that practice onto the existing diagnostic, closing *"the gap between the diagnostic infrastructure and the practice infrastructure."*

**First task at open — ground the design in the mentor's own words.** The plan's one-line descriptions are a starting hypothesis, NOT the design. Read the survey's principle-6 AND principle-15 sections in `inbox/Mentor answer to remaining principles question.rtf` (search `### 6` — "The oikeiosis extension — the five circles"; and `### 15` — "Cosmopolitanism — the world city") and confirm the mentor's design:

- **#6 circle-extension exercise (verbatim):** *"presents a current decision or situation and asks the practitioner to identify which circle they are currently reasoning from, then to explicitly reason from the next circle outward, and to notice what changes in the action assessment when the circle expands."* CRUCIAL FRAMING: **"This is not a diagnostic — it does not produce a verdict. - **#15 cosmopolitan obligation check (verbatim, folded into #6):** *"After the practitioner has extended their reasoning to the fourth circle, the check asks: what obligations of citizenship does this circle generate, and are any of them engaged by the current action?
