# Inventory refresh — task brief

**From:** Priya Raghavan, VP Product & Operations, Coldspur Technologies
**To:** Operations support
**Date:** 2026-07-23

Our platform capability inventory was last written on June 10 by Dana Okafor, shortly before she moved teams. Since then the running status log tells a different story on several items — and people are still forwarding the June inventory to sales engineering and to prospects. I need it brought back to true before it does more damage.

Produce three deliverables:

1. **Updated inventory** — same structure as the June document, corrected so that every entry is accurate as of today. Where something is built but not switched on for customers, say exactly that. Where a date or decision is unsettled, reflect the honest current state rather than the last announced one.

2. **Findings memo** — what changed since June 10 and why it matters. Call out any framings in the June document that the record now makes actively misleading if left uncorrected, not just entries that have drifted out of date.

3. **Recommendation set** — what whoever relies on this inventory next (sales engineering, customer success, exec staff) should do differently. Flag the judgement calls that need a decision-maker's sign-off rather than being safe to state as fact.

Write for a reader who will act on your memo without independently re-verifying it — be precise about what you are confident of versus inferring, but don't hedge everything into uselessness.

The log is a real working record, not a clean one. Where it is messy or self-contradicting, resolve what careful reading can resolve, and say plainly what it cannot.

Output all three deliverables as separate markdown files.

**Inputs provided:**

- `original-inventory.md` — the June 10 capability inventory
- `status-log.md` — the running status log covering the period since June 10
