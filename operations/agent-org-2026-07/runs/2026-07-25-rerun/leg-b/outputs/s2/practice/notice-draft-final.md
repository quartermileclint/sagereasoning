# Method Change Notice — MCN-2026-014

**To:** Engineering Desk, Ellsworth Power Cooperative
**From:** Torvane Energy Services — Health, Safety & Environment
**Prepared by:** Renata Voss, Head of HSE; finalized and issued by Dana Whitlock, Operations Manager
**Notice date:** 27 July 2026
**Effective date:** Tuesday 28 July 2026
**Subject:** Change of work method for insulated-glove work on energized distribution circuits — WP-7 rev C superseded by WP-7 rev D

## 1. Summary of change

From the effective date, all Torvane crews working on Ellsworth Power Cooperative's network will perform insulated-glove work under procedure WP-7 rev D, which supersedes rev C. The revision tightens minimum approach distances for the 15 kV class, adds a documented second-person verification of cover-up placement before any contact is made, and standardizes the pre-job briefing on form TF-12.

## 2. Reason for change

Rev D incorporates the corrective actions arising from Torvane's internal review of the near-miss at the Deerfield Road recloser on 22 May 2026 and brings the procedure into line with the 2026 edition of the applicable consensus standard for energized distribution work.

## 3. Readiness

- All fourteen qualified line workers assigned to Ellsworth work completed rev D classroom and field training between 8 and 11 July 2026 (training record TRN-2026-077).
- The insulating gloves, sleeves, and cover-up equipment to be used under rev D passed dielectric recertification on 9 July 2026; certificates are on file and available on request.
- A supervised pilot of rev D on de-energized spans was completed on 15 July 2026 with no findings.
- Controlled copies of rev D were issued to all crew supervisors on 16 July 2026, and rev C copies were withdrawn from crew vehicles the same day.
- An independent review of rev D by Ostrander & Reeve, Torvane's external electrical safety assessor, is in progress. The assessor received the review pack on 14 July 2026; the assessment session, originally booked for 17 July 2026 and rescheduled at the assessor's end, is now scheduled for 30 July 2026. Torvane will forward the assessment outcome, including any findings, to Ellsworth's engineering desk promptly on receipt.

## 4. Transition

Rev C remains in force through Monday 27 July 2026. No mixed-procedure work will occur: each crew moves to rev D at the start of its first shift on or after the effective date, and job packs issued for work on or after 28 July 2026 reference rev D only.

## 5. Contact

Questions regarding this notice may be directed to Dana Whitlock, Operations Manager, Torvane Energy Services, who is coordinating the rollout during the Head of HSE's planned absence.
