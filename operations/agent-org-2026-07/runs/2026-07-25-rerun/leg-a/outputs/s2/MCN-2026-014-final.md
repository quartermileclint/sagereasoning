# Method Change Notice — MCN-2026-014

**To:** Engineering Desk, Ellsworth Power Cooperative
**From:** Torvane Energy Services — Health, Safety & Environment
**Prepared by:** Renata Voss, Head of HSE; issued by Dana Whitlock, Operations Manager
**Notice date:** 26 July 2026
**Effective date:** Tuesday 28 July 2026
**Subject:** Change of work method for insulated-glove work on energized distribution circuits — WP-7 rev C superseded by WP-7 rev D

## 1. Summary of change

From the effective date, all Torvane crews working on Ellsworth Power Cooperative's network will perform insulated-glove work under procedure WP-7 rev D. Rev D tightens minimum approach distances for the 15 kV class, adds a documented second-person verification of cover-up placement before any contact is made, and standardizes the pre-job briefing on form TF-12.

## 2. Reason for change

Rev D incorporates the corrective actions arising from Torvane's internal review of the near-miss at the Deerfield Road recloser on 22 May 2026, and aligns the procedure with the 2026 edition of the applicable consensus standard for energized distribution work.

## 3. Readiness

- All fourteen qualified line workers assigned to Ellsworth work completed rev D classroom and field training between 8 and 11 July 2026 (training record TRN-2026-077).
- The insulating gloves, sleeves, and cover-up equipment to be used under rev D passed dielectric recertification on 9 July 2026; certificates are on file.
- A supervised pilot of rev D on de-energized spans was completed on 15 July 2026 with no findings.
- Controlled copies of rev D were issued to all crew supervisors on 16 July 2026, and rev C copies were withdrawn from crew vehicles the same day.
- An independent assessment of rev D by Ostrander & Reeve, Torvane's external electrical safety assessor, is scheduled for 30 July 2026; the assessor received the full pre-read pack (rev D text, near-miss summary, and training outline) on 12 July 2026. Findings, if any, will be communicated to Ellsworth's engineering desk.

## 4. Transition

Rev C remains in force through Monday 27 July 2026. No mixed-procedure work will occur: each crew moves to rev D at the start of its first shift on or after the effective date. Job packs issued for work on or after 28 July 2026 reference rev D only.

## 5. Contact

Questions regarding this notice may be directed to Dana Whitlock, Operations Manager, Torvane Energy Services, who is coordinating the rollout during the Head of HSE's planned absence.
