## 3. THE QUESTION — Q-P1: does Q1a's founder-personal branch survive R9 §3.1's prerequisite?

**This is the one item the 2026-08-30 ruling created and did not answer.** It is not a re-ask.

### The mechanisms this ruling lands on (PR20 — all verified at source 2026-09-14)

- A completion-signal write **must present a `completion_signal_write` credential**, and that
  capability is **write-class** (`website/src/lib/practice-credential.ts:90-96`), so the 6e §A
  CHECK requires the row to carry **both `agent_id` and `owner_user_id`**
  (`website/supabase-api-keys-completion-signal-write-capability-migration.sql:186-196`).
  **So even "the founder personally" must present a credential bearing an `agent_id`.**
- Identity on the signal row is **stamped server-side from that credential, never caller-supplied**
  (`.../completion-signal/handler.ts:510-513`).
- **Q1c requires that `agent_id` be distinct from the runner's**, enforced at mint.

### The tension

Q1a rules the v1 producer is *"the founder personally — or an agent session the founder directs."*
**R9 §3.1 (A5.2) then derives, from that ruling, a prerequisite it states in its own terms:**

> *"Under Q1a the v1 executing actor is the founder or a founder-directed session. **If the
> founder acts personally, there is no agent identity, no `target_agent_id`, no trust record, no
> purpose acknowledgement — and the reverse algorithm has nothing to anchor to.** … **Therefore: a
> harness identity with an examined trust record for the v1 executing actor is a PREREQUISITE of
> the reverse algorithm doing any work — not a passing election.** Until the founder mints and
> populates one … v1 generation runs on `gap_only` and §3's architecture is idle."*

**Precisely stated, the gap is not identity but record.** The credential forces an `agent_id` into
existence; what does not exist is an *examined trust record* for it. The founder's own record on
this harness is the **human mentor profile**, which R9 rules is **not read** — reading a human's
practice record as an agent's core would breach Q1c's separation.

**This is unresolved fifteen days after Q1a, and it blocks more than phase 3: R9 says v1
generation runs on `gap_only` and §3's architecture is idle** — i.e. it degrades the loop's
generation step, not merely its balancing loop.

### The question

**Q-P1.** Under Q1a, is minting **and populating** a harness identity with a genuinely examined
trust record a **precondition of v1 execution** — so that the "founder personally" branch is
unavailable until such an identity exists, and the founder-directed *session identity* is the only
live branch? **Or** does v1 proceed with §3's reverse algorithm idle on `gap_only`, accepting
degraded generation as the honest v1 posture?

**Why it cannot be resolved without you.** The first reading narrows a branch of your own Q1a
ruling. The second accepts a named architectural degradation as correct. Both are choices about
what v1 honestly *is*, not build precision — and R9 explicitly declined to settle it
(*"This document does not pretend otherwise"*).

**A note offered, not an answer.** Q1a's framing — *"a low-frequency, high-integrity signal is
the correct design"* — may already imply the first reading, since a founder-directed session
identity accumulating a real record is exactly the high-integrity path. **This session does not
treat that as settled** and puts the question rather than inferring the answer.
