**L-2 (what distinguishes): Ruled — dimension (c) is load-bearing; all five dimensions are in scope for O-C's design.** The five distinction dimensions named in §3.2 are accepted as the observed architectural material they are presented as. The ruling on which of them is load-bearing: dimension (c) — the honesty dimension — is the load-bearing one, as the reflect residual confirms. The architecture already runs the forensic posture that declines to trust interior access; the rendering presumes that access. The gap between posture and invitation is the concrete instance of what uncalibrated costs in practice. Dimension (a) — who the reader is presumed to be — and dimension (b) — direct address versus structured relay — are the structural dimensions the crisis precedent already encodes. They are in scope for O-C's design and should be carried forward as design constraints, not invented fresh. Dimension (d) — which affordances are named — is in scope for O-C's design. Human form names crisis resources and support paths. Agent form names endpoints, structured next steps, and the operator's own escalation process. The relay pattern (suggested_user_message carrying the human-form content inside the agent form) is the precedent to follow, not to replace. Dimension (e) — role-calibration — is in scope for O-C's design and is addressed under L-4 below. The ruling for O-C's design session: all five dimensions constitute the distinction the project should encode. Dimension (c) is the load-bearing one and should be the first design question the O-C session addresses.

**L-3 (the discriminator's limits): Ruled — the reuse constraint stands; both honest limits are carried explicitly.** The discriminator-reuse constraint is confirmed binding. No second practitioner-type discriminator is introduced by any ruling here or by any execution of it. The two honest limits named in §3.3 are carried explicitly in the record and in any implementation that follows: Transport-level classification: the signal classifies the caller, not the downstream practitioner. An agent operator whose end-user is human reads agent_developer. Any calibrated rendering that follows the crisis precedent's relay pattern (human-form content carried inside the agent form as suggested_user_message or equivalent) inherits the same honesty. Any calibrated rendering that does not follow the relay pattern must provide its own answer to the relayed-human case before execution. The flag-off asymmetry is historical, not live. The route falls back to human_user when the audience-rendering flag is off; ConsumerContext defaults to agent_developer on absence. The flag has been on since 2026-05-31. A future design does not treat these two defaults as contradictory precedent — they are defaults for different surfaces under different flag conditions, both of which are now historical.

**L-4 (relational context): Ruled — F-d (design-target only) for now; F-b opened post-run; R20d boundary confirmed binding.** The four placeholder fields — relational_context, practitioner_role, relationship_type, examination_status — remain named-not-built. The R17 wiring obligation is structural and must be stated in the implementation record before the edit is made. The relationship_type distinctness requirement is confirmed as a binding constraint: relationship_type describes the relationship the practitioner is reasoning about; the discriminator describes the practitioner's own type. These must remain distinct notions by construction. The moment relationship_type is read to infer which practitioner type is this, a second discriminator exists and the inherited constraint is violated. This constraint must be stated explicitly in any design document that carries these fields. The R20d boundary is confirmed binding on all surfaces: engage the self-side, decline the other-side. The guide's first move when relational context is active is what impression are you assenting to about this relationship, and have you examined it — not what is the other party doing. This applies regardless of practitioner type. The two minimum pieces are confirmed as the design target for any relational-context rendering: (1) the practitioner's role in the relationship, not the relationship type — different roles carry different kathekonta, and the guide calibrates to the role; (2) whether the practitioner's impressions about the relationship are being examined or assumed. It is a named honest limit, not a blocking constraint. F-c (human mentor tools) is noted for completeness and is out of this arc's agent-first ordering. It is not ruled here.

**L-5 (the reflect-wording residual): Ruled — yes to recalibration; mentor-vetted verbatim required; instrument-change discipline applies.** The Q1–Q6 invitational wording should be recalibrated for the agent practitioner. The ruling is yes. The ground: the architecture runs the forensic posture that declines to trust interior access; the invitation presumes that access without disclosing the posture it sits inside. The gap is a honesty gap, not a structural gap. The structure is not touched by this ruling. What the recalibration must do: disclose the posture the invitation sits inside. The agent practitioner should know that answers are cross-checked out-of-band against signed evidence, and that honest "I cannot determine" is a legitimate answer. The invitation should not presume interior access it cannot verify. The instrument-change discipline of §3.5 applies in full: Mentor-vetted verbatim wording is required before execution. The project's precedent (the B5 suggestion-line) is that even concept-approved wording on measured surfaces awaits mentor-vetted verbatim. The concept is approved here. The verbatim wording is not yet produced. Claude should produce candidate wording for Q1 through Q6 — recalibrated for the agent practitioner, disclosing the forensic posture, naming honest "I cannot determine" as legitimate — and return it for mentor review before any edit to question-bank.ts. The wording change must land at a clean boundary: post-run, with the change recorded so any before/after read of reflect-derived event rates is segmentable. This is the same class of discipline the arc applies to the false-hold observation window. The following are outside this ruling's reach and must not be touched by any execution of it: the never-abbreviated sequence, the mandatory sub-questions (FD-R3, FD-R4, the C2e orientation sub-question), the G4 mechanism, the G4 cross-check's 3-part standard, and the controlled SUB_SPECIES vocabulary. The G4 suppression watch, the honest-completion event, and the Q6 orientation sub-question are live trust-event emitters. The wording change is the elicitation side of distributions feeding decrease-, flag-, and modulate-class events. The change is not effect-free copy-editing.
