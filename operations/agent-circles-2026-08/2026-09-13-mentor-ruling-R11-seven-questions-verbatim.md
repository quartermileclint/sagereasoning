## Seven questions ruled — R11 design deliverables

---

**Q-R11-B1 — Dethronement reading: floor only (i) or worst-draw proximity re-enters the election (ii)?**

Reading (i) is ruled. The sampling layer dethroning a would-be winner requires a floor. A floor-free
sampled winner is not dethroned by an unsampled survivor whose single draw read higher. The session's
recommendation is correct and the grounds are sound. Q-M5 stated the layer's purpose as catching
winners that carry a latent floor — the floor, not the rank. Reading (ii) compares a minimum over K
draws against single draws on one ranking axis, which is the shape the D6a rulings removed when they
struck pooled figures that imply a regularity the data does not carry. A K-draw minimum is
systematically deflated relative to a single draw on the same distribution. Ranking them against each
other on the election axis would disadvantage the sampled candidate by construction, not by evidence.
That is not what a floor is for. The worst-draw proximity still flows on the existing backward edge
per R9-D10. It does not re-rank a floor-free winner. The build brief carries reading (i).

**Q-R11-B2 — Incomplete series under W: hold or stand with disclosure?**

Hold. When the sampling layer cannot obtain K verdicts for the would-be winner, no verdict is issued
until K verdicts are in hand. The gate's published outage posture is conservative pause — never a
silent proceed. The sampling layer is an extension of the same instrument and must apply the same
logic. A partial series under W is not a completed measurement. The worst-draw rule requires K draws
to operate; fewer than K draws is not a worst-of-K verdict, it is an incomplete attempt. Issuing a
verdict on fewer than K draws and disclosing the incompleteness would be issuing a verdict the policy
does not license. The distinction from the Option S measurement disposition is real and the session
names it correctly. Option S was measurement — the ruling there was disclosure because the question
was empirical and the data was directional. This is a policy that changes a recorded verdict. The
conservative direction is the doctrinal one. Hold until K verdicts are obtained. Disclose the hold and
its cause. Do not issue a partial verdict.

**Q-R11-A4 — Measurement vehicle: bounded re-run or wait for the standing runner?**

A bounded, founder-attended re-run is admitted as the measurement's vehicle. Q-M7's discipline is
telemetry-first before designing the policy's parameters. The standing runner's stream is the intended
population and does not exist. Waiting for it to exist before measuring is waiting for the policy to
be built before measuring what the policy should be designed against — the wrong sequence. A bounded
re-run under the same conditions as the validation run (20–40 cycles, founder-attended,
early-run/signal-producing split) produces a live-loop population in the relevant sense: verdicts from
the actual engine on actual candidates, not from the closed Option S population. The election of
whether to commission a founder-walked run prompt is the founder's. The ruling is that a bounded
re-run satisfies the measurement's vehicle requirement. Deliverable A rides whichever loop the founder
next runs, as stated.

**Q-R11-A1 — First-draw signals as triggers: admissible only on a measured relation?**

A trigger is admissible only on a measured relation between the signal and the latent floor. The
Prerequisite Criterion governs. A trigger computed from a signal whose relation to variance is
unmeasured produces an output resembling confidence without the prerequisite. The scoping draft ruled
this against itself in advance, and the ruling confirms it. No first-draw signal — katorthoma
proximity, is_kathekon, proximity_floors.basis, or any corroboration finding — may serve as a trigger
condition until Deliverable A's cross-tabulation exists and shows a measured relation between that
signal and whether the input carried a latent floor. After the cross-tabulation exists, the question
returns with evidence. If the table shows no relation, no trigger is admissible from that signal. If
it shows a relation, the trigger is admissible on that signal with the relation disclosed. The table
is the prerequisite. The ruling does not pre-empt what the table will show.

**Q-R11-A2 — Surface scope: gate only or does sampling reach the consult path?**

Gate only. The sampling policy does not reach the consult path at this stage. The measurement is
scoped to /api/guardrail. The policy is designed against that measurement. The consult path — from
which the trust ledger accumulates credential-completed — is a second population with a second
credential shape. The published disclosure already states that no rate has been measured on
/api/reason and this one does not transfer to it. Extending the policy to the consult path before
measuring that population would be applying a policy designed against one population to a different
population without a measured basis. That is the same move the Prerequisite Criterion forbids for
triggers. If the consult path comes into scope in future, it is a separate design with its own
measurement, not an extension of this one.

**Q-R11-A3 — The reserved instrument defect's sequencing: must it land before any new measurement runs?**

The defect does not bind the new capture by construction. Deliverable A's measurement may proceed
independently. The reserved correction — changing complete_series() to count counted outcomes rather
than records — applies to the existing Option S instrument in option-s/. Deliverable A is a new
capture, runner-side, with the verdict-count completeness requirement stated in its own design from
the outset. A new instrument that correctly defines completeness as counted outcomes from the start
does not inherit the defect from the old instrument. The sequencing constraint was: the correction
must be made by a session that does not know which input it affects. That constraint applies to the
option-s/ instrument. It does not apply to a new instrument that never had the defect. R11 is
correctly disqualified from the option-s/ fix. The fix is reserved for a future session that has not
read the Option S close and rulings. That sequencing is unchanged. Deliverable A proceeds
independently.

**Q-R11-C1 — ATRF item-3 wording: which is ruled, and is "whether the idea was completed" retained?**

The full wording is ruled. The phrase "whether the idea was completed" is removed. The full wording is
cleaner, more precise, and correctly states what the signal carries without ambiguity about what
"completed" means. The minimal wording retains "whether the idea was completed" as a struck-through
phrase with replacement text, which leaves the completion reading available as a residue. The full
wording eliminates it entirely and states the signal's content affirmatively: the executing agent's
examination of its own assent, whether that assent was examined or habitual, whether the threshold
reached was katorthoma or kathekon, with a refuse-to-attest branch, carrying no task-outcome content
and no comparison of outcome to proposal. "Completed" as succeeded is not in the signal — R8 §2
carries no success/failure indicator. "Completed" as executed at all is attested by the signal's
existence, not by a field in the signal. The full wording correctly omits the word and lets the
signal's existence carry the execution fact. The minimal wording's retention of the phrase, even
struck through, preserves an ambiguity the full wording resolves. The application to manifest.md is
the founder's own act after this ruling.
