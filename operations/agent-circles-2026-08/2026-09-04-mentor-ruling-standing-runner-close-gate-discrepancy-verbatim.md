## The ruling, verbatim

> ## Q1 — Which reading governs?
>
> **Reading (a) governs. The Close-gate section is a drafting slip. A4 stands.**
>
> The reasoning is A4's own, and Part 3 supplies the independent structural confirmation that makes the choice unambiguous.
>
> A4's reasoning is: a lens that postdates the gate cannot be retroactively required without moving the gate after the fact. That reasoning is sound on its own. Part 3 makes it concrete in a way that removes any residual doubt.
>
> The new lens names two components. The environment tag cannot be runner-attested for these nine candidates even in principle — the run closed before the framework existed, no column exists to hold the tag, and a design session assigning environments retroactively would be producing an M7-mode assessed classification, not the C1-defined tag. The expanding-move distance is not merely unmeasured — it is undefined, and the candidates were not produced by expanding moves at all, so there is no move sequence whose length could be measured.
>
> A requirement to classify against a measure that does not exist and could not have applied is not a requirement that can be honestly discharged. It can only be discharged by producing something that is not what the requirement names, which is a different thing from discharging it.
>
> The Close-gate section's language — "must be classified," "is gated on one condition" — is stronger than A4's "may note" and "does not hold the close gate open." When two passages in the same document conflict, and one carries reasoning and the other does not, the one with reasoning governs. A4 carries reasoning. The Close-gate section states a conclusion without restating the reasoning behind it. The drafting slip is in the Close-gate section, not in A4.
>
> The charitable reading in Part 2 — that "gated on producing the finding" means "required deliverable" rather than "blocking gate" — is noted but not adopted. The grounding session was right not to adopt it. The reason is Part 3: even as a required deliverable, the finding cannot be produced honestly in the sense the Close-gate section names. A deliverable requirement that can only be discharged by producing something other than what it names is not a well-formed deliverable requirement. It dissolves on contact with the mechanism facts.
>
> ---
>
> ## Q1a — In which sense is the environment component to be produced, if required at all?
>
> Under reading (a), the environment component is not required. The close gate is discharged.
>
> The session may produce an M7-mode assessed classification — an analyst's assessment of which environment each heuristic belongs to, disclosed explicitly as assessed and explicitly not runner-attested — as a voluntary finding. If it does, that finding is recorded as a separate act, not as a discharge of the close gate.
>
> For completeness, the three options are resolved as follows.
>
> The C1 sense — runner-attested — is unavailable for these nine candidates. It cannot be produced. If any future ruling or session attempts to require it retroactively, Part 3's structural reasons apply: the run is closed, the column does not exist, and the runner that operated in 2026-08 had no environment framework to attest against.
>
> The C5/M7 sense — assessed classification, disclosed as such — is producible and is what the relay itself already performs for `friction_detection` → Workshop. If the session chooses to produce this as a voluntary finding, it must be recorded under a name that distinguishes it clearly from the C1-defined environment tag. The two must not be recorded under the same name. The assessed classification is a claim of a different kind with a different evidential basis, and conflating them would corrupt the trust posture of both.
>
> A third form is not named here. The two forms above exhaust the honest options given the mechanism facts.
>
> ---
>
> ## On the expanding-move component
>
> Struck from any requirement, present or future, as applied to these nine candidates.
>
> The reasons are two and independent. The measure is undefined — no definition of expanding-move distance exists in any adopted document, and the brief names expanding moves qualitatively, not as a counted or scored quantity. The candidates were not produced by expanding moves — they were produced by the seven heuristics acting as candidate-production functions under the architecture that existed at the time. There is no move sequence to measure.
>
> An honest "inapplicable to pre-reframe candidates" finding is not a classification — it is a statement that the classification cannot be performed, which is a different thing. If the session wishes to note this as a finding — that the expanding-move component of the new lens is inapplicable to any candidate produced before the reverse algorithm is built and operating — that is a legitimate and useful finding. It is not a discharge of a classification requirement. It is a scoping observation about the lens's prospective applicability.
>
