### What this instruction asks Claude to examine

The 2026-08-20 brainstorm session produced a structured analysis of what the ancient Stoics would do
with new findings — specifically, whether findings warrant evolution of the existing deterministic
engine, boundary revision of who and what the engine applies to, precision of the engine's existing
procedures, or spinoff of a variant engine optimised for a different problem class. The analysis
produced four candidate directions. Claude is asked to examine each direction against the existing
engine architecture, name what each direction would actually require at the level of the engine's
components, identify where the directions are well-grounded and where the structural account is
absent, and produce a named design document that holds the findings honestly — as conjectural entries
where the structural account is not yet available, not as settled conclusions.

This is an examination task, not a build task. The output is a document that can be routed as a
scoped question when it is ready. It is not itself a scoped question.

---

### The four candidate directions — as brought from the brainstorm

These are stated as the brainstorm produced them. Claude's task is to examine them, not to accept
them as given.

**Direction 1 — Extension of the existing engine: adding a generative capacity account.**

The existing engine diagnoses what happens when an impression arrives and is evaluated. It does not
account for what happens when the hegemonikon generates new impressions from the examination of
existing ones — the curiosity loop, the conjectural entry, the incubation entry. The brainstorm
proposed that the engine needs a complementary account of the generative mode alongside the
evaluative mode. The virtue questions this opens: what does phronesis look like in the mode of
discovery rather than evaluation? What does andreia look like when the agent holds a conjectural
entry in the probably-true state without manufacturing an explanation? What does sophrosyne look like
when the agent must resist the impulse to close an open question prematurely?

Claude is asked to examine: is this a genuine gap in the engine, or does the existing passion
diagnosis and kathekon framework already account for the generative mode implicitly? If it is a
genuine gap, what specifically would need to be added — a new diagnostic sequence, a new virtue
domain application, a new category of impression? What is the structural account, and where is it
absent?

**Direction 2 — Boundary revision: refining the account of who and what the engine applies to.**

The engine's current boundary condition is rational agents. The animal cognition findings and the LLM
reinforcement learning findings suggest that rationality is not a binary property but a capacity that
admits of degrees. The brainstorm proposed a functional definition: the engine applies wherever the
capacities to receive impressions, examine them, and grant or withhold assent are present, calibrated
to the degree of capacity present. The Sage Assent has already begun this extension. The question is
whether the boundary revision requires a change to the engine's components or only to the conditions
under which the engine is applied.

Claude is asked to examine: does the graduated rationality finding require a pre-Stage-1 account in
the oikeiosis framework, or does the existing five-circle structure accommodate it? Does the passion
diagnosis framework require modification when applied to agents with partial rational capacity, or
does it apply as-is with outputs calibrated to capacity? What is the structural account of how the
engine's outputs change as rational capacity varies, and where is that account absent?

**Direction 3 — Precision of the existing engine: incorporating the epistemic status framework.**

The engine's existing diagnostic procedures produce outputs that carry implicit epistemic statuses —
the passion diagnosis is an inference, the oikeiosis reading is an inference, the kathekon assessment
is an inference. The epistemic status framework (GS-ATRF-4) proposes making those statuses explicit
and governing them with the rule that confidence must never exceed evidential basis.

This is connected to GS-ATRF-4 as a forward-pointing note, not a pre-answer. The ATRF scoping
session will examine GS-ATRF-4 in its proper context. This direction concerns the engine's own
diagnostic procedures, not the ATRF's proposal shape.

Claude is asked to examine: which of the engine's existing outputs are observations, which are
inferences, which are assumptions, and which are genuinely unknown? Where does the engine currently
produce outputs that carry more confidence than the evidential basis warrants? What would change in
the engine's diagnostic sequence if each output were required to carry a formal epistemic status? Is
this a precision of the existing engine or a structural revision of it?

**Direction 4 — Spinoff: a variant engine optimised for the generative mode.**

The brainstorm proposed that the generative capacity account may be better served by a spinoff engine
than by extension of the existing engine, because the mode of inquiry is structurally different from
the mode of evaluation. The existing engine is primarily a practical syllogism engine — moving from
principles to action. The spinoff would be a theoretical syllogism engine — governing the movement
from observations to conclusions in a way consistent with Stoic epistemic discipline. The puzzle
taxonomy and the curiosity loop are the beginning of this spinoff. The three entry types (inductive,
conjectural, incubation) are its first structural components.

Claude is asked to examine: is the spinoff genuinely a different engine, or is it the same engine
applied to a different input class? What components of the existing engine carry over to the spinoff
unchanged, which require modification, and which have no equivalent in the spinoff? What is the
relationship between the spinoff engine and the existing engine — are they parallel, sequential, or
hierarchically related? Where is the structural account available and where is it absent?

### The governing constraint on this examination

The brainstorm named the honest epistemic status of the four directions: probably true, confidence
basis is the convergence of the historical analysis with the existing engine's architecture,
structural account pending. Claude's examination should honour that status. The output document
should distinguish clearly between:

- Findings where the structural account is available and the direction is well-grounded
- Findings where the structural account is partially available and the direction is probably right
- Findings where the structural account is absent and the direction is a conjectural entry — probably
  true, held honestly without a manufactured explanation

The governing rule applies throughout: confidence of an explanation must never exceed its evidential
basis.

### What the output document should contain

The output is a named design document — not a scoped question, not a governance ruling, not a build
instruction. It should contain:

**1. An examination of each of the four directions** — structured as above, with the structural
account stated where available and explicitly marked absent where not.

**2. A named finding for each direction** — one of: well-grounded (structural account available),
probably right (structural account partially available), conjectural (structural account absent, held
as probably true), or not supported (examination found the direction does not hold).

**3. A cross-reference map** — which directions connect to which existing governance items (GS-ATRF-1
through 4, the Consciousness and Continuity Obligation, the Sage Assent, the puzzle taxonomy), stated
as forward-pointing notes not pre-answers.

**4. Named open questions** — what the examination found it could not answer, stated honestly as
unknown rather than approximated.

**5. A proposed routing** — for each direction that is well-grounded or probably right, which session
in the Q11 sequence is the correct destination for a scoped question, and what the scoped question
would need to establish. For conjectural directions, the routing is: held in this document until a
session is positioned to receive it.
