## 3. THE TWO QUESTIONS R13 COULD NOT SETTLE

### Q-R13-A — does R13-D1's reading of "the correct direction" hold?

**The ruling:** *"The additional constraint the brief names — the runner being required to record its
own engagement with the target before the anchor is served — is not ruled as mandatory at this stage,
but it is the correct direction for the design to develop toward. **If the runner's generation step is
designed to include an explicit examination of the anchor's disclosed limitations before reasoning
from it, that examination is the prerequisite the envelope cannot supply by itself.**"*

**R13-D1 reads those two sentences as naming different things** — the first a *recording*, the second
an *examination* — and concludes that the examination is the prerequisite while a recording of it, on
its own, is a self-report the harness cannot credit. **On that reading the correct direction is real
and is runner-side, and the server's contribution is a content constraint it can actually check.**

**The alternative reading, which R13 does not take and may be wrong to refuse:** that the recording
requirement *is* the direction, to be built server-side now, with its unverifiability accepted as the
cost of making the expectation explicit.

**Why R13 cannot settle it:** the two readings differ on whether an unverifiable recorded claim is
worth building. **This project's standing posture says no; your sentence says the direction is
correct.** R13 resolved that tension by locating the direction in the runner, which is a
substantive reading of your words and not a neutral one. **If it is wrong, R13-D1 is overturned and
should be.**

### Q-R13-B — is R13-D3's verdict admissible ahead of the examination your Q5 ruling assigned?

**The 2026-09-13 Q5 ruling:** *"The role-relative evaluation session opens first. **Q-M5's
would-be-winner scope interacts with the total gap on role input, and that interaction should be
examined.**"* — assigned to the **role-relative evaluation session**, not to this track.

**R13-D3 clears Q-M5's would-be-winner scope against the single-backward-edge constraint on a
structural argument, without touching that interaction.** It is not a missed deliverable — the
examination is not this track's. **But R13-D3's verdict reads more cleanly than the record supports**,
and R13 records that as an honest limit rather than letting it stand unqualified.

**The question: does R13-D3's topological verdict stand on its own, or must it be held until the
role-relative session has examined the interaction?** R13 takes no position.
