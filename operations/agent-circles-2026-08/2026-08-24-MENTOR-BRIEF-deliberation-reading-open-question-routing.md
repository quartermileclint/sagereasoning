# Mentor brief — routing the deliberation-reading doctrinal open question (§5d)

**Authored 2026-08-24.** `governance`, documents only. **Licenses no code.** Nothing here asks to
open the doctrinal question as a build item — the ruling's *"do not let the published note stand as
the resolution"* is honoured by **anchoring**, not by designing.

**What is being asked:** the 2026-08-24 ruling directed that the doctrinal question be carried *"as a
scoping question for the generation-step or standing-runner design sessions, where the deliberation
reading's architecture is already in scope."* On verification, **that premise does not hold for
either named home.** This brief relays the finding rather than picking a home.

**The question being routed (map §5d, OPEN):** does a ruling faculty deliberating in the
control-filter, value-assessment, or causal-stage mechanisms — but not in the oikeiosis mechanism —
count as deliberating, in the Stoic sense? The ruling's own answer is not neutral: the *hegemonikon*
is not partitioned by mechanism, so the oikeiosis-only reading is a **proxy**, disclosed as such on
both published notes (§5b, §5c, live).

## 2. A prior binding ruling may already determine this, without a new one

**`2026-08-19-mentor-ruling-late-arriving-carry-forward-ruled-session-verbatim.md`** established a
general principle for exactly this class — a carry-forward pointed at a session that has already
closed. It ruled: **not void, and not a dated amendment to the closed generation-step document**;
instead **redirected to the next unopened session in the Q11 sequence whose subject matter is the
right home, recorded as a named input, not a pre-answer** — and the redirect *"does not constitute
opening the standing-runner design session early."* Its worked case used the generation-step document
as the wrong home and the **standing-runner design** as the right one.

Applied to §5d, that principle appears to route on its own terms, and its own test for shape (a) is
met here: the generation-step document's subject matter is the generation step's build decisions, and
§5d is *"formally correct and practically inert"* there because the session is closed and does not
discuss the reading. **This also weakens one half of the finding above:** the standing-runner
document's non-existence is not a blocker — a forward reservation as a named input is the sanctioned
mechanism, not a fallback.

**Whether the 2026-08-19 principle governs a doctrinal open question raised by a ruling (as opposed
to a carry-forward drafted by a session) is not for us to decide.** It is put here so the routing can
be confirmed under existing principle or explicitly overridden.

## 4. Mechanism facts the routing lands on (PR20), verified first-hand today

- **One predicate, two readers, deliberately** (`layer2-mechanisms.ts:1569`, in terms): the oikeiosis-only
  deliberation predicate is read by the `ruling_faculty_state` branch **and** by `computeProximityBase`.
- **So the build target named in both notes' lifetimes** — *"until the deliberation reading reflects
  the full ruling faculty's deliberative state across all mechanisms"* — **is a change to the live
  deterministic scoring path.** `katorthoma_proximity` floors gate verdicts, feeds `proximity_floors`,
  and drives trust events; widening the predicate's input set is engine-class, `code-critical`,
  flag-gated, PR19-reviewed work, not a disclosure change.
- **By contrast, what the hegemonikon-drift record's ruling actually landed is disclosure- and
  read-side:** R-2 a `does_not_attest` item; R-3 the dispersion signal on **M-B only**, MEASURE-only,
  *"never on the public trust record"* (M-C ruled out). **Routing §5d there would place an
  engine-class build target in a home whose open work is disclosure-class.** Whether that matters is
  the mentor's call; it is named rather than absorbed.

## 5. The question of principle we are not answering

**Is the oikeiosis-only deliberation term and the zero-variance-certifies-`advanced` finding ONE
defect class or two?** If one, the routing follows automatically and a single scoping session should
hold both. If two, they need separate homes and the parallel is coincidental.

We deliberately do not answer it. The shape-vs-substance distinction is live: both are proxies whose
label overreaches their input, remedied by the same disclosure machinery — but the **remedies differ
in kind.** §5d is an *under-read*: the fix is to widen the input set, and the sources it fails to read
already exist in the same snapshot. R-1 is an *under-determination*: reading more of what is already
there cannot fix it, because low variance is consistent with two causally distinct states, and
distinguishing them requires perturbation the record's own population may never contain (the
survivorship limit R-5 carried as a named honest limit). One is tractable by architecture; the other
may not be tractable at all. The mentor has ruled on one half of this already.
