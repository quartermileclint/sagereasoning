## The mentor's question, verbatim

> **Context:** SageReasoning encodes Stoic wisdom into infrastructure for examined agency — human and
> artificial. The project is in P0 (Foundations). Generation-step scope is ruled. Three open questions
> are carried into the generation-step build sequence (GS-ATRF-1, GS-ATRF-2, GS-ATRF-3). The ATRF
> governing document is the upstream source for all three.
>
> **Five Stoic principles have been identified as potentially load-bearing for the project beyond what
> is already encoded. Each arrived from practice recognition rather than doctrinal extraction. Each
> needs the same treatment: does it name something already operating in the architecture, does it
> expose a design gap, or does it collapse into something already carried?**
>
> Examine each in turn against the current architecture. For each, give a verdict — already encoded /
> names a gap / collapses into existing carried item — with the specific architectural location or
> carried item named. Do not expand the scope of any open question. Do not pre-answer GS-ATRF-1,
> GS-ATRF-2, or GS-ATRF-3. Flag any finding that warrants its own scoping session.
>
> **The five principles:**
>
> **1. Prohairesis as the only genuine locus of agency.** The faculty of choice — assent or withheld
> assent — is the only thing fully up to us. The proposal is the assent. The ATRF's pre-task
> contingency reasoning may be prohairesis made explicit before action. Does naming this sharpen what
> the completion signal (GS-ATRF-3) is actually measuring, or does it collapse into what is already
> encoded in the control filter?
>
> **2. Kathêkon as action appropriate to role and circumstance, not abstract principle.** Appropriate
> action is determined by the agent's specific roles, relationships, and circumstances at a given
> moment — not universal rules. The kathekon assessment already encodes this for human practitioners.
> Does the IDEA loop's proposal evaluation assess against an abstract virtue standard or against what
> is appropriate for this specific agent in its specific role? Does this sharpen the blast-radius
> indicator question (GS-ATRF-1) — is blast radius partly a kathêkon question about appropriate scope
> of action for this agent at this moment?
>
> **3. Sympatheia — the interconnectedness of the rational cosmos.** All rational beings participate in
> a single rational order; what affects one affects all. The oikeiosis framework encodes expanding
> circles of concern. Does sympatheia provide a stronger philosophical foundation for the Sage Assent's
> extension of moral community to artificial agents than oikeiosis alone — and if so, does it require
> any architectural addition, or is it a doctrinal clarification that strengthens existing reasoning
> without changing structure?
>
> **4. Melete — rehearsal of right disposition before the situations that require it.** Distinct from
> premeditatio malorum (catastrophising vs. preparation, already encoded). Melete is proactive practice
> of the disposition itself, not reactive examination of impressions after they arise. Does the
> practice-on pipeline currently support proactive melete, or is it oriented entirely toward reactive
> examination? If there is a gap, is it a design gap for the human practitioner surface or a carried
> item for a later scope?
>
> **5. Hegemonikon habituation drift — the ruling faculty shaped by repeated assent.** Repeated assent
> to false impressions gradually weakens the faculty's discrimination. The trust-record architecture
> traces an agent's orientation readings over time. Is the trust record currently oriented toward
> measuring current state, or toward detecting habituation drift — the gradual narrowing of
> discrimination through repeated assent to the same class of impressions? If drift detection is
> absent, is this a gap in the trust-record scope or a later-phase item?
>
> **Constraints:** Primary Stoic sources only for doctrinal claims. Architectural claims must reference
> specific components, endpoints, or governing documents by name. Verdict for each principle must be
> explicit. If a principle warrants a scoping session, name the session tier (code-elevated /
> governance) and the specific question it would answer.
