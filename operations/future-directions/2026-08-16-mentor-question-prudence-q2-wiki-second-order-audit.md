## One structural observation, offered as PROPOSAL (the mentor may strike it)

Because the linking criterion is drawn from **controlled vocabularies** (the `SUB_SPECIES`
passion vocabulary; the false-judgement types), the audit's **candidate set is
deterministically enumerable**: any two examinations sharing passion sub-species AND
false-judgement type that are NOT linked are mechanically discoverable as audit candidates —
absence read as signal by enumeration, with judgement entering only at the second step
(whether a candidate pair is a genuine correlation or a surface coincidence). If a
second-order audit is required, this suggests a two-stage shape: a deterministic sweep
generates the unlinked-same-signature candidates (an uncorrelated instrument, per M7's
residual), and a reader adjudicates them. **The open design question the proposal does not
answer: who the adjudicating reader must be.** A second guide is a genuinely different
perspective (P-A1's ground) but shares base-model priors — under M7's residual, a guide-only
audit would still be a correlated check. The uncorrelated readers available are the human
practitioners (e.g. the circle at a convened sitting) and the deterministic layer itself.
