**MENTOR RULING — Prudence Group Q2: findings wiki second-order audit**

**Date:** 2026-08-15.

---

**Q2-1 (whether): The wiki requires the second-order audit.** The record's framing is correct and the stake is real. A wiki that only links what the guide notices is a wiki that reflects the guide's limitations, not the community's truth. The philodoxia failure mode named in the question — a guide systematically failing to link examinations that would reveal its own recurring false judgements — is not a hypothetical. It is the exact failure mode the architecture's forensic posture (L4 passion audit, out-of-band cross-check) was designed to catch on the consult surface. The wiki surface has no equivalent check. The two-guide structure plus the deterministic instruments are not sufficient on their own, because the linking step is performed by the guide and the guide's priors are the failure mode. Recording the limitation instead of building the check would be honest but insufficient — the limitation is structural and the mechanism to address it is available. The audit is a required structural component of the wiki's design that Stage-3 scoping must carry.

---

**Q2-2 (shape): Option (i), with the two-stage structure as proposed.** The structural observation offered as a proposal is accepted and adopted. The two-stage shape is correct: Stage 1 is deterministic: enumerate all examination pairs sharing the same passion sub-species and false-judgement type that are not currently linked. This is a mechanical sweep over controlled vocabularies — the candidate set is fully enumerable without judgement. This stage is an uncorrelated instrument in the sense your M7 residual names: it does not share the guide's priors because it does not reason; it computes. Stage 2 is adjudicative: a reader examines each candidate pair and determines whether the shared signature reflects a genuine correlation (the same distorted phantasia operative in both matters) or a surface coincidence (same vocabulary, different underlying structure). Judgement enters only here. The two stages must remain structurally separate. A design that collapses them — where the guide performs the sweep and the adjudication in sequence — reintroduces the failure mode at Stage 1. The sweep must be performed by the deterministic layer, not by the guide.

---

**Q2-3 (who reads): A combination, with the deterministic layer performing Stage 1 and human practitioners as the primary adjudicating reader at Stage 2.** The guide as sole adjudicating reader at Stage 2 is insufficient. The M7 residual is explicit: the two-guide constraint prevents reciprocal-validation corruption but does not prevent correlated blind spots arising from shared base-model priors. A second guide adjudicating the unlinked-same-signature candidates is a genuinely different perspective on the surface question (is this pair correlated?) but shares the base-model priors that generate the philodoxia failure mode in the first place. A guide-only audit at Stage 2 is a correlated check on the dimension that matters most. The uncorrelated readers available are the human practitioners and the deterministic layer. The deterministic layer alone cannot adjudicate Stage 2 — the question of whether a shared signature reflects a genuine correlation requires reasoning about the specific examinations, not just their vocabulary tags. Human practitioners are the primary adjudicating reader at Stage 2 for the following reasons: they are the community whose truth the wiki is meant to reflect; they are uncorrelated with the guide's priors on the specific examinations they participated in; and their adjudication of whether two examinations share the same underlying distorted phantasia is itself a form of philosophical examination — it is not an administrative task delegated to them but a substantive contribution to the wiki's integrity. The combination that satisfies the uncorrelated-check requirement: the deterministic layer performs Stage 1 (the sweep); human practitioners adjudicate Stage 2 (the correlation determination), with the second guide available as a supplementary reader where no human practitioner has participated in both examinations being compared. The second guide's correlated-priors residual is named explicitly in the wiki's own documentation wherever a guide-adjudicated link appears.

---

**Q2-4 (sequencing): Folds into Stage-3 scoping session inputs. Nothing gates now.** The record's own note is confirmed: nothing currently blocks on Q2. The ruling folds into the Stage-3 scoping session's inputs when that session opens. The scoping session carries the following as settled inputs from this ruling: The second-order audit is a required structural component of the wiki's design, not an optional enhancement. The two-stage shape (deterministic sweep + adjudicative reader) is the required form. The deterministic layer performs Stage 1; human practitioners are the primary adjudicating reader at Stage 2; the second guide is a supplementary reader where no human practitioner has participated in both examinations, with the correlated-priors residual named in the wiki's documentation. The linking criterion (passion sub-species + false-judgement type, not topic similarity) is confirmed as the correct basis for both the sweep and the adjudication. The Stage-3 scoping session does not re-open any of these four points. It designs the mechanism that implements them.
