### The heuristic — what it is and what it is not

The cross-check you have found is real and it is better than nothing. When kathekon reads contrary,
checking whether the other arrays — passions present, oikeiosis circles engaged, value categories at
stake, causal-stage evidence — are all empty versus some populated is a genuine signal about
extraction quality. All empty looks like a general extraction failure. Some populated suggests the
extractor engaged substantively and specifically found nothing kathekon-relevant. That distinction is
derivable now, with no server change, and it is honest about what it is.

But name it precisely: it is a confidence proxy, not a discriminant. It raises or lowers the
probability that a contrary reading is genuine. It does not resolve which case occurred. A rich
extraction elsewhere does not prove the kathekon dimension was examined correctly — a genuinely
missed factor could hide behind an otherwise-rich extraction. You have stated this honestly and the
honest statement is the load-bearing part of the question.

---

### The ruling on opening 2

**The heuristic is not an acceptable basis to arm opening 2.** The reason is not the residual
false-negative risk alone. The reason is what opening 2 does with the signal.

Opening 2 arms the existing elicitation machinery from the Gate-2 consult's verdict — it fires a
reflect turn on every distinct decision where the verdict is adverse. The firing rate is meaningfully
higher than the guard's allowlist. At that frequency, a confidence proxy with a known false-negative
channel is not a filter — it is a rate-limiter on false positives that still passes false positives
through. The elicitation fires on some genuine adverse verdicts and on some sparse-extraction defaults
that happen to have rich extraction elsewhere. The practitioner cannot distinguish which is which. The
instrument's credibility erodes on the same axis the item-4 ruling identified, just more slowly.

The condition I named for opening 2 was: a genuine second-pass check specifically on the kathekon
dimension, or a new structured wire field. The heuristic is neither. It is a proxy derived from
adjacent dimensions, not a check on the dimension itself.

**Opening 2 stays held.**

---

### The ruling on opening 3 phase two

**The heuristic is an acceptable basis to arm opening 3 phase two, with one constraint on how it is
used.**

Opening 3 fires once per session at the close hook. The frequency is fixed regardless of what the
heuristic returns. The heuristic's role in opening 3 phase two is not to gate a firing — it is to vary
the content of a firing that already occurs. That is a different use of the same signal, and the
difference matters.

At close-hook frequency, a confidence proxy that is disclosed as such — never claimed as certain, only
as graded — is an acceptable basis for content variation. The close turn already fires. The question
is whether the content it carries reflects something the session actually produced. A high-confidence
adverse verdict — contrary reading with rich extraction elsewhere — is a better basis for
session-specific content than a low-confidence one — contrary reading with all arrays empty. The
heuristic makes that distinction. **The content variation should reflect the confidence level:** a
high-confidence adverse verdict warrants session-specific content naming the kathekon dimension; a
low-confidence one warrants either generic content or no variation at all.

**The constraint:** the content variation must disclose the heuristic's basis when it fires on a
high-confidence adverse verdict. Not a technical disclosure — a plain one. Something in the close
turn's content that signals this reading came from a session where the extractor engaged
substantively and found nothing kathekon-relevant, rather than a session where the extractor may have
missed something. The practitioner should know the difference between "the instrument found something
worth reflecting on" and "the instrument thinks it found something but cannot be certain." That
distinction is the honest use of a confidence proxy.

**Opening 3 phase two is unblocked on this basis.** The content variation design should carry the
confidence level explicitly, not absorb it silently.
