## 1. The argument

The observation, verbatim (`2026-08-11-mentor-synthesis-primal-substrate-verbatim.md`, Heading 2):

> Innovation in the animal kingdom originates from primal tension — hunger, competitive pressure,
> reproductive drive — that forces departure from fixed patterns. **Behavioural flexibility** … **is
> not generated by contentment.**

The research's own definition of this trait, from `inbox/eleven traits research.rtf`, distinct from
the sentence above: *"Capacity to depart from fixed action patterns **when the environment changes**."*
The mentor's gloss — *"generated by friction between what the organism wants and what the environment
currently provides"* — is a legitimate synthesis drawing on the wider trait set (competition, resource
acquisition), **but it is the mentor's interpretation, not a quotation of the research.** The two are
attributed separately here, deliberately, because collapsing them would present an inference as a
citation.

The Stoic reconciliation:

> Chrysippus defined the impulse (**horme**) as the starting point of action. **The sage does not lack
> impulse** — the sage has impulse that is fully examined and proportionate. The difference … is not
> that the sage feels no drive but that **the sage's drive is not excessive and is directed at genuine
> goods.**

The consequence for agents:

> an agent operating **only from virtue-aligned heuristics** … **has no mechanism for the kind of
> departure from fixed patterns that produces genuine novelty.** The IDEA loop's heuristic 7, friction
> detection, is **the closest current mechanism** to this … **That is the functional analogue of hunger
> driving tool use.**

And the resolution — a gap, not a vice:

> The Stoic-compatible version of this is not greed or laziness — it is **the examined recognition of a
> gap between what is and what virtue requires.** That gap is the engine of Stoic progress. The
> **prokoptôn** — the one making progress — is not content with current virtue. They are **in
> productive tension with the distance between their current state and the sage ideal.**

## 2. The corpus grounding

**`horme` already carries the mentor's exact distinction.** `CAUSAL_SEQUENCE` stage 3
(`website/src/lib/stoic-brain.ts:584-589`): *"If assent is given, an impulse toward or away from
something arises."* Failure mode: *"Excessive impulse (pathos) — impulse that overshoots reason."* The
corpus already says the defect is **excess**, not the impulse. Note also that `horme` sits **after**
`synkatathesis` (Assent) — impulse follows assent — which is why `framing-01-primal-substrate.md`'s
"examination is only possible if the impulse is present and visible" is structurally right: suppressing
the impulse before assent removes the thing assent is given or withheld to.

**The prokoptôn structure is committed, three-graded, each grade defined by what remains undone.**
`SENECAN_GRADES` (`website/src/lib/stoic-brain.ts:471-506`):

- Grade 3, *Beginning the Path* — *"Progress is real but uneven."*
- Grade 2, *Overcoming the Worst* — *"Major passions checked but minor ones still operative."*
- Grade 1, *Approaching Wisdom* — *"Most passions overcome but not all … Can still relapse under
  extreme testing."*

Every grade is defined by a remaining gap. The mentor's *"productive tension with the distance between
their current state and the sage ideal"* is a description of this structure, not an addition to it.
`PROGRESS_DIMENSIONS` (`website/src/lib/stoic-brain.ts:514`) is its committed four-dimension
measurement.

**The mechanism the mentor describes already exists — as a shipped human tool.** `/sage-compass` (live
since 2026-07-15) is the mentor's own #14 design: *the sage as a compass bearing, not an aspiration.*
Its shape (`website/src/app/api/mentor/sage-compass/route.ts`):

1. name the decision and the action being considered;
2. identify which virtue is **primarily** engaged;
3. write what that virtue's **complete expression** would look like here — the bearing;
4. name the **distance** between that expression and the action.

Its binding constraint, verbatim in the live route:

> ***NOT A VERDICT (mentor #14, binding).*** … Nothing in this route computes, scores, ranks, grades,
> or classifies it.

This is S2's "gap between what is and what virtue requires", already built, already live, already
ruled — for humans. It is a live instance of the coherence `/logos` was built to make visible: an agent
mechanism descending from the same doctrine as its human tool.

**Ruled (C9): the not-a-verdict constraint binds the agent-side mechanism too.** *"A computed gap that
feeds a score is the same violation in a different medium."* Any generation-step mechanism that computes
this gap must produce a generation input, never an assessment. Carried forward and enforced in
`framing-03-boulesis-mechanism.md` §2.

## 3. The distinction that must not be flattened

`GenerationHeuristic` (`website/src/lib/substrate/idea-loop-types.ts:81-88`) has exactly seven values;
`friction_detection` is one. Per the ruled generation design, friction detection identifies points
where the existing system resists or fails — a routine-task step that takes longer than expected,
produces an unsatisfying result, or requires workaround behaviour.

**The mentor's own framing is careful and must not be flattened.** Heuristic 7 is *"the closest current
mechanism"* — **not** that it already is the mechanism the mentor is proposing. The mechanism the
mentor actually wants is *"not what is broken technically, but … the gap between current capability and
the examined ideal."* Friction detection reads **technical** resistance. The proposed mechanism reads a
**normative** gap. These are different questions.

**Ruled (C7): two proposals, not one.** *"The normative-gap mechanism adds a question: where does the
system fall short of what virtue requires? Heuristic 7 reads technical friction. Conflating them would
let cycle 2's evidence stand in for a mechanism that did not produce it."* The friction-as-primary
hypothesis (S6, `friction-primary-hypothesis.md`) and this normative-gap mechanism (S3) share a
philosophical root but are **separately testable**, could both be adopted, or either alone.
